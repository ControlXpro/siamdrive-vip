Don Mueang, met and moving

Don Mueang is Bangkok's original airport and today the hub for many regional and low-cost airlines. It is busy, efficient and closer to the city's northern districts than Suvarnabhumi, and a private chauffeur turns a crowded arrivals hall into a calm exit.

DMK25 km · 0.5–1 hrs to centreMeet & greetFlight tracked

Two terminals, one meeting point

Don Mueang has two connected terminals: Terminal 1 for international flights and Terminal 2 for domestic services. Your chauffeur or meeting host waits in the arrivals hall of the correct terminal with a sign bearing your name, and we confirm the exact meeting point on WhatsApp before you travel.

Many travellers arrive at Don Mueang from regional hubs or Thai islands with checked luggage, shopping and sometimes dive or golf equipment. Your chauffeur helps with every bag, and our Alphard and Vellfire MPVs take four large suitcases with ease.

If you are travelling with children, older relatives or someone who needs extra help, tell us when you book. Ask about a child seat at the same time, and your chauffeur will make sure the car is as close as practical to the terminal exit.

We monitor your flight

Low-cost schedules can shift at short notice. Share your flight number when you book and we monitor your flight, adjusting the pick-up time if you land early or late. You do not need to send updates; just message us on WhatsApp once you have your luggage.

Arrivals halls at Don Mueang can be crowded in the evening, when several regional flights land close together. Having a named chauffeur waiting saves the scramble for the public taxi rank or a ride-hailing pick-up point.

Into the city

Central Bangkok is approximately 25 km from Don Mueang, and the journey usually takes around 30 minutes to an hour. Your chauffeur generally uses the elevated Don Mueang Tollway above Vibhavadi Rangsit Road and then the expressway network, which bypasses much of the surface traffic.

Don Mueang is particularly convenient for Ari, Chatuchak, Ratchada and the northern business districts, and is well placed for journeys north to Ayutthaya, Lopburi or Khao Yai without crossing the city. The SRT Red Line now links the airport to Bang Sue, but a private car still offers the only true door-to-door option with luggage.

Departures and assistance

For departures, your chauffeur collects you with time to spare and drops you at the right terminal. Budget airlines often close check-in strictly, so we plan pick-up times generously during peak hours.

Our fast-track and VIP buggy add-ons are currently offered at Suvarnabhumi. If you would like assistance at Don Mueang, ask on WhatsApp and we will tell you what can be arranged for your flight. Every transfer is a fixed price with fuel, tolls and the chauffeur included; see the rate table below.

Highlights

  • Meet and greet at Terminal 1 or 2
  • We monitor your flight, early or late
  • Don Mueang Tollway into the city

Insider tips

  • Check whether your flight uses Terminal 1 or 2 and share it when booking.
  • Budget airlines close check-in strictly; leave extra time for departures.
  • Evening arrivals are busiest; your named chauffeur saves the taxi queue.
  • Travelling on to Suvarnabhumi? Allow a generous connection; see our inter-airport transfer.

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Where do I meet my chauffeur at Don Mueang?

In the arrivals hall of your terminal, Terminal 1 for international and Terminal 2 for domestic flights. We confirm the meeting point on WhatsApp.

How long does it take to reach central Bangkok?

Usually around 30 minutes to an hour for approximately 25 km, using the Don Mueang Tollway where possible.

Is fast-track available at Don Mueang?

Our fast-track and buggy add-ons are offered at Suvarnabhumi. Ask on WhatsApp about options for your Don Mueang flight.

Can you take me straight from Don Mueang to Pattaya or Hua Hin?

Yes. We run direct transfers to both, without stopping in central Bangkok.
