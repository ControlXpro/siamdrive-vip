U-Tapao, the gateway to the east

U-Tapao Rayong–Pattaya International Airport sits on the Eastern Seaboard between Pattaya and Rayong, a far shorter hop to the beaches than either Bangkok airport. Your chauffeur meets you at arrivals and has you on the coast road within minutes.

UTP175 km · 2.5 hrs to centreMeet & greetFlight tracked

A small airport, close to the coast

U-Tapao operates from a compact terminal shared with a Royal Thai Navy airbase in Sattahip district. It handles a smaller schedule of domestic and regional flights, along with charters, so arrivals are generally calmer and quicker than at Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang.

Your chauffeur waits in the arrivals area with a sign bearing your name. We confirm the meeting point on WhatsApp before you fly, and your luggage is loaded for you at the car.

Where travellers are heading

Most U-Tapao arrivals are bound for Pattaya, typically around 40 minutes to an hour away depending on your hotel, from Jomtien in the south to Wong Amat in the north. Sattahip, Bang Saray and Nong Nooch Tropical Garden are closer still, often just 15 to 30 minutes from the terminal.

Heading east, Rayong's beaches and the industrial estates around Map Ta Phut are typically within about 30 to 45 minutes, and Ban Phe Pier for Koh Samet is a little further along the coast. For business travellers visiting the Eastern Economic Corridor, U-Tapao can save several hours compared with flying into Bangkok.

Golfers are well served too: several of the Eastern Seaboard's best-known courses lie between Pattaya and Sattahip, and your chauffeur can take you straight from the terminal to the first tee if your flight lands in the morning. Villa guests in the hills behind Jomtien and Na Jomtien also find U-Tapao the most convenient arrival point.

Flight monitoring and timing

Share your flight number when booking, and we monitor your flight. If it lands early or late, your chauffeur adjusts accordingly. With fewer flights, schedules at U-Tapao can change seasonally, so we always confirm details with you on WhatsApp a day or two before travel.

For departures, the small terminal means you need less time than in Bangkok, but we still recommend arriving at least two hours before an international flight. Your chauffeur will plan the pick-up from your Pattaya or Rayong hotel accordingly.

Onward to Bangkok

If you are continuing to the capital, central Bangkok is approximately 175 km away, around two and a half hours via Motorway 7. It is a comfortable drive in an Alphard or Vellfire, with fuel, tolls and the chauffeur included in one fixed price.

All U-Tapao transfers are priced from the rate table below. Fast-track and VIP buggy services are a Suvarnabhumi add-on; at U-Tapao, the short walk from gate to kerb rarely calls for them.
