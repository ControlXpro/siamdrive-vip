Bangkok District · Phahonyothin & Soi Ari

Ari, Bangkok's Leafy Village

Ari is a quiet, low-rise neighbourhood north of Victory Monument, known for its independent cafés, small restaurants and leafy residential lanes. It feels like a village within the city, and it is perfectly placed for northern Bangkok and Don Mueang Airport.

A neighbourhood of cafés

Soi Ari, officially Phahonyothin Soi 7, and the lanes that branch from it are filled with cafés, bakeries and restaurants in converted houses. Government offices nearby bring a lunchtime bustle, while the evenings are relaxed and local.

The best spots are scattered across sois that are narrow, with little or no parking. A chauffeur drops you at the door, waits nearby and takes you to the next café or gallery without the long, hot walk. La Villa Ari, beside the BTS, is a convenient meeting point.

Ari is also a good base for exploring the wider north: the green spaces of Chatuchak Park and Wachirabenchathat Park, the Children's Discovery Museum and the vast Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal at Bang Sue all lie within a short drive. A chauffeur strings them together easily.

Close to the north of the city

Ari is only a short drive from Chatuchak Weekend Market and Or Tor Kor, one of Bangkok's finest fresh markets. Victory Monument and Saphan Khwai are nearby, and the IMPACT Muang Thong Thani arena, which hosts concerts and exhibitions, is reachable by road.

Don Mueang Airport is easily reached via the tollway, making Ari a smart base for travellers using domestic and regional flights. An early-morning pick-up from your residence means you can be at the terminal with time to spare.

Coming from Suvarnabhumi instead? A direct airport transfer to Ari is straightforward via the expressway network, and we can arrange fast-track at the terminal so you are on your way quickly after landing.

Why a chauffeur helps

Phahonyothin Road carries heavy traffic at rush hour, and taxis are scarce in the inner sois. If you are visiting several places in the area, or heading across the city to Sukhumvit or the riverside, a chauffeur saves time and energy.

For longer-term residents, a monthly private driver makes daily life simpler, from school runs and shopping to evenings out. Visitors who prefer a guide can add a personal assistant, who can recommend cafés, translate and help with errands.

If you are staying in Ari for work, a ten-hour chauffeur on your busiest days means you can move between ministries, offices and meetings across the city without waiting for taxis or walking in the heat.

Choosing your service

A five-hour chauffeur covers a café afternoon or a Chatuchak trip. A ten-hour booking suits a day that also includes the city centre. Airport transfers to Don Mueang and Suvarnabhumi are available at any hour, and fast-track can be added at Suvarnabhumi. Guests attending large concerts can add a bodyguard for the crowds.

Local tips

  • Many Ari cafés are busiest at weekends; visit on a weekday for calm.
  • Phahonyothin is congested from 7 to 9 am and 5 to 8 pm.
  • Give your chauffeur the café's map pin; addresses can be confusing.
  • Visit Chatuchak early in the morning to avoid the heat and crowds.
