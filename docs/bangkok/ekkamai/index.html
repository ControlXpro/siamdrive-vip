Ekkamai, at Your Own Pace

Ekkamai sits just east of Thonglor and shares its love of good coffee and good food, but with a more relaxed, residential feel. It is popular with families, creatives and anyone who wants space to breathe in Sukhumvit.

Hourly from 8,000 THBAirport 5,000 THB24/7

A neighbourhood of cafés and courtyards

Ekkamai Road, or Sukhumvit Soi 63, runs north from the BTS station towards New Phetchaburi Road. Along it and its sub-sois you will find independent cafés, design shops and restaurants tucked into converted houses, as well as the Park Lane and Gateway Ekamai malls.

The best spots are scattered and often well away from the BTS. A chauffeur lets you hop between a late breakfast, a gallery and an afternoon café without the long walk in the heat, and the car is waiting whenever you are ready to move on.

Ekkamai is also a practical base for the eastern side of the city, with quick access to the motorway towards Suvarnabhumi and the coast. It is easy to combine a relaxed local morning with meetings elsewhere, and your chauffeur can advise the best time to set off.

Easy days for families

Families based in Ekkamai appreciate a private car more than most. The Science Centre for Education, next to the Eastern Bus Terminal, is popular with children, and international schools and playgrounds are spread across nearby Sukhumvit.

An Alphard offers space for strollers, car seats on request and room to rest between activities. Your chauffeur keeps water cold and the air-conditioning ready, so the children step out of the heat and straight into a cool car.

Older travellers appreciate the same comforts: a driver who opens doors, helps with steps and bags, and drops you as close to the entrance as possible. It removes most of the small frictions that make a hot, busy city tiring.

Skip the bus to the coast

The Eastern Bus Terminal at Ekkamai is where many travellers catch buses to Pattaya and the eastern islands. A private transfer leaves from your door instead, with luggage handled for you, stops whenever you want and no fixed timetable.

We arrange direct transfers from Ekkamai to Pattaya, Rayong and Ban Phe pier for Koh Samet, as well as day trips with the car waiting at your destination.

Travelling as a larger party? The Alphard and Vellfire carry up to five passengers with four large bags, and we can arrange a second vehicle for extra luggage or guests.

The right booking

A five-hour chauffeur is ideal for a café-and-shopping afternoon, while a ten-hour booking suits a family day across the city. A personal assistant can plan the itinerary and handle bookings, leaving you free to enjoy the day.

Staying in Ekkamai for several weeks? Ask about a monthly private driver, which gives you a dedicated chauffeur for school runs, errands and evenings out.

Local tips

  • Ekkamai Road is two-lane in places; evening traffic builds from about 5 pm.
  • Let us know in advance if you need child seats for your booking.
  • Weekday mornings are the quietest time for café visits.
  • For coastal trips, leave before 7 am to beat traffic on the motorway.
