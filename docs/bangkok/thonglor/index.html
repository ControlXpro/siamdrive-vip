Thonglor After Dark, Made Easy

Thonglor is Bangkok's most fashionable address for dining and late nights, a long soi lined with chef-led restaurants, cocktail bars and boutique malls. It is also famously hard to get around once evening traffic builds.

Hourly from 8,000 THBAirport 5,000 THB24/7

One soi, three kilometres long

Soi Thonglor, officially Sukhumvit Soi 55, runs from the Thong Lo BTS station all the way north to New Phetchaburi Road. Its best restaurants and bars are spread along its length and down its numbered sub-sois, often a long walk from the station.

From around 7 pm, cars queue in both directions and parking is scarce. Ride-hailing pick-ups frequently fail because drivers cannot stop. A chauffeur who knows the sub-soi shortcuts, including the lanes that link through to Ekkamai, gets you to dinner on time and collects you at the door.

The soi also runs through an established Japanese neighbourhood, with supermarkets, clinics and restaurants serving long-term residents. During the day, school runs and deliveries make it busier than its relaxed reputation suggests, so even a lunchtime appointment benefits from a driver who knows where to stop.

Eating and drinking well

Thonglor's dining spans Japanese izakayas, contemporary Thai tasting menus and casual open-air spaces such as The Commons, a community mall full of food stalls and cafés. Boutique malls like J Avenue and 72 Courtyard add bars and restaurants that stay busy until late.

With an hourly chauffeur you can move between them without a second thought. Have an aperitif at one venue, dinner at another and a nightcap somewhere new, with the same car and driver waiting each time.

If you would like help choosing, our personal assistant service can suggest restaurants to suit the occasion and help with reservation requests, subject to availability. Popular tables are often booked days ahead at weekends, so it is worth planning early.

The safe way home

Thonglor's evenings often stretch past midnight. Rather than flagging down a taxi on a busy street, you message your chauffeur, who brings the car to the entrance. It is the most comfortable and responsible way to enjoy a night out.

For a celebration with friends, the Alphard's lounge seating keeps everyone together, and the Porsche Cayenne S makes a confident statement. Prominent guests can add a bodyguard who accompanies them on foot. Your chauffeur keeps the car cool and ready, and waits quietly for as long as your evening lasts within the booking.

Which booking fits

A five-hour chauffeur suits a dinner-and-drinks evening. A ten-hour booking works well if you are spending the afternoon at a spa or the nearby Samitivej Sukhumvit Hospital, then heading out for the night.

Arriving at Suvarnabhumi? An airport transfer can take you straight to a serviced residence in Thonglor, often in well under an hour outside peak traffic, with fast-track available at the terminal to shorten your arrival.

Local tips

  • Book dinner for 7:30 pm or later to avoid the worst of the evening queue.
  • Give the sub-soi number with your restaurant address; Thonglor is long.
  • Some venues enforce smart dress after dark; check before you go.
  • Agree a pick-up spot with your chauffeur before you head into a busy bar.
