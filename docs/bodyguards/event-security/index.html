Personal Protection for Memorable Evenings

Large occasions bring crowds, late finishes and a great deal happening at once. A SiamDrive bodyguard looks after you and your party specifically — from the moment the car arrives to the moment you are home — while the venue's own team looks after the event.

From 5,000 THB / guardSuited · English-speakingBooked with your car

Your detail, not the venue's

Hotels and event venues employ their own security to manage doors, guest lists and the building. That is not what we provide. Your SiamDrive bodyguard is a personal detail: their attention is on you, your partner or your guests of honour, not on the room as a whole.

They introduce themselves to the venue's security team on arrival so that everyone understands who they are and why they are there. The two roles work comfortably side by side, and you benefit from both.

If you are hosting, they also become a useful extra pair of hands: confirming that a late guest of honour has arrived safely, or that a car is ready for an older relative who wishes to leave early.

Knowing the venue in advance

Bangkok's ballrooms, riverside terraces and rooftop venues each have their own geography. Before the event, your bodyguard and chauffeur work out the drop-off point, the route from the kerb to the room, any holding area for principal guests and the quickest way out if you wish to leave early.

Where possible, the bodyguard arrives ahead of you to walk the route. It is unglamorous work, but it means you are never led down a service corridor that turns out to be locked, or left waiting on a crowded forecourt.

Seating is worth considering too. A table with a clear line to an exit, and a spot nearby where your bodyguard can stand without intruding, is easy to request when the plan is made early.

Through the evening

Your bodyguard dresses to the occasion — a dark suit by default, adjusted to match a black-tie gala or a wedding dress code — and positions themselves at the edge of your group rather than in the middle of it. They stay out of photographs unless you want them in, keep an eye on bags and jewellery set down at the table, and quietly steer you away from conversations you would rather not have.

If a guest becomes overfamiliar or the room grows crowded, the response is calm and courteous: a word, a gesture, a change of position. The aim is that very few people notice anything happened at all.

Getting everyone home

Events tend to end all at once, and the forecourt fills with cars. Your chauffeur is told in advance when you are likely to leave, and your bodyguard confirms the car is at the door before walking you out. For weddings moving between ceremony and reception, several vehicles can travel together with motorcycle escort riders.

Celebrations in Pattaya or Hua Hin are covered too, with a supplement for assignments outside Bangkok. Message us on WhatsApp with the date, venue and guest list size, and we will suggest a proportionate detail. We cannot promise any event will be free of incident, but we can promise careful preparation.

A typical day

    Venue walk-through

    Your bodyguard arrives early to check the drop-off point, the route to the room and the exits, and agrees the plan with your chauffeur.

  2. 18:30

    Your arrival

  3. 19:00

    Your bodyguard stays at the edge of your group, out of photographs, watching the room and your belongings.

  5. 23:00

    Home safely

    You are accompanied to the car and on to your hotel or residence door before the detail stands down.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Do you provide security for the whole event?

No. We provide personal protection for you and your party. Venue and door security remain the responsibility of the venue or organiser, and your bodyguard works alongside them.

Will the bodyguard appear in our photographs?

Only if you wish. They position themselves at the edge of your group and step out of frame for photographs.

What will the bodyguard wear?

A dark suit by default. Tell us the dress code — black tie, cocktail or a themed wedding — and we will brief accordingly.

Can you cover a wedding outside Bangkok?

Yes. Weddings in Pattaya, Hua Hin and elsewhere are welcome, with a supplement for assignments outside Bangkok included in your quote.

Can several guests have protection?

Yes. We can arrange several bodyguards and vehicles for a larger party, planned together so everyone arrives and leaves in good order.
