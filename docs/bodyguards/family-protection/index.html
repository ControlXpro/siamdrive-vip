An Extra Pair of Watchful Eyes

Bangkok is a wonderful city for families, and a busy one. A SiamDrive bodyguard adds a calm, attentive adult to your group — someone whose only job is to keep track of everyone — while your chauffeur takes care of getting you from place to place.

From 5,000 THB / guardSuited · English-speakingBooked with your car

Another trusted adult beside the children

Parents in a crowded mall have a great deal to manage: tickets, bags, lunch plans, and children who wander towards whatever catches their eye. Your bodyguard is not a nanny, but an extra pair of eyes focused on your family. They keep a running head count, stay between the children and the busiest flow of people, and help steer a small group through queues and escalators.

Before setting off, a meeting point is agreed at each venue and the bodyguard notes what everyone is wearing — simple habits that make a busy day much easier to manage.

Teenagers who want a little independence can be given it with a light touch: your bodyguard keeps them in sight at a respectful distance rather than at their elbow.

Shopping and attractions, planned

Whether the day includes Siam Paragon, ICONSIAM, an aquarium or a weekend market, your bodyguard and chauffeur plan the drop-off points, the entrances and the times when crowds are lighter. Large venues often have several car-park levels and entrances, and choosing the right one avoids a long walk with tired children.

When you are ready to move on, the car is called forward and waiting as you arrive, so nobody stands at the kerb in the heat while it is found.

Lunch stops and rest breaks are built into the plan, along with quieter corners of large malls where a young child can take a moment away from the noise.

Hotel movements and split plans

Families rarely move as one all day. One parent may have a meeting while the other takes the children out, or teenagers may want an afternoon of their own. We can arrange a bodyguard and car for each group, coordinated so everyone knows where the others are.

Around the hotel, your bodyguard accompanies movements through lobbies and to the car, and can wait nearby during meals or at the pool if you wish. Because protection is always booked together with a SiamDrive vehicle, the whole day runs from one plan.

If a tired child needs to return early, we can arrange a second car so part of the family heads back while the rest of the day continues as planned.

Calm, friendly and discreet

Our bodyguards wear suits by default, though for a theme park or a hot day outdoors smart, practical clothing may suit better — tell us and we will brief accordingly. They are introduced to the children at the start, and most young travellers quickly treat them as a reassuring part of the trip.

Tell us about your family and your plans on WhatsApp, including any house rules you would like respected, and we will suggest a sensible arrangement. Day trips outside Bangkok carry a supplement. No one can guarantee a day without mishaps, but an extra watchful adult makes them far less likely and far easier to handle.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Is the bodyguard a babysitter?

No. They are a security professional focused on keeping your family together and safe in busy places. Parents remain responsible for childcare.

Will the children feel uncomfortable?

Rarely. Your bodyguard is introduced at the start, is friendly and calm, and most children quickly see them as part of the trip.

Can we have protection for two groups at once?

Yes. We can arrange a bodyguard and a car for each group, coordinated so everyone stays in touch.

Will the bodyguard wear a suit at a theme park?

Suits are the default, but for outdoor days we can brief smart, practical clothing that suits the setting.

Can the bodyguard join day trips outside Bangkok?

Yes. A supplement applies for assignments outside Bangkok and is included in your quote before you confirm.
