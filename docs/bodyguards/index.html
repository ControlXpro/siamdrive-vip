Who your bodyguard is

Our bodyguards are suited, English-speaking security professionals chosen for their composure and discretion. They dress to blend into a hotel lobby or a boardroom, keep a watchful eye on your surroundings and handle the small practicalities — doors, crowds, the route from car to entrance — so you can focus on your day.

Their role is calm, attentive presence. Most clients find that a bodyguard is noticed less than they expected, yet makes a real difference to how relaxed they feel.

Before the assignment begins, your bodyguard is briefed on your schedule, the venues you will visit and anything you would like handled in a particular way — who may approach you, where you prefer to sit, whether photographs are welcome. What you share stays between you, your bodyguard and your chauffeur.

Always booked with your car

Bodyguards are only available together with a SiamDrive car booking. That is deliberate: your chauffeur and bodyguard work from the same plan, know the pickup points and timings, and communicate throughout the day.

The result is a single, coordinated team rather than two separate suppliers trying to keep pace with each other.

In practice, your chauffeur positions the car while your bodyguard checks the entrance; when you are ready to leave, the car is already at the door. Small moments like these are where coordinated security quietly earns its place, and they are far easier to achieve when everyone works for the same team.

Flexible lengths of booking

Choose a bodyguard for a single transfer — an airport arrival, for example — or for a five- or ten-hour block that matches your chauffeur hire. Additional hours are charged at the overtime rate, and a supplement applies for assignments outside Bangkok. All rates are listed in our rate table.

For convoys and high-profile movements, bodyguards can also work alongside our motorcycle escort riders, who travel ahead of the vehicles to keep the group together through busy junctions.

When clients ask for protection

Visiting public figures, executives travelling with sensitive materials, families who want an extra adult beside the children, and guests attending high-profile events all book bodyguards with us. So do travellers who simply prefer to feel secure in an unfamiliar city, particularly late at night.

If you have specific concerns, share them privately on WhatsApp and we will advise on the right arrangement. Sometimes that means one bodyguard for a single evening; sometimes it means a small team, a second car and escort riders for a multi-day visit. We will recommend what the situation genuinely calls for, and nothing more.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Can I book a bodyguard without a car?

No. Bodyguards are only available together with a SiamDrive car booking, so the security and driving are planned and run as one service.

Do your bodyguards speak English?

Yes. All of our bodyguards speak English, and they are comfortable working in formal business settings as well as busy public places.

How will the bodyguard dress?

Suited, by default. If your day calls for something different — resort wear in Hua Hin, for example — tell us and we will brief accordingly.

Can a bodyguard come with me outside Bangkok?

Yes. A supplement applies for assignments outside Bangkok, and it is included in your quote before you confirm.

Can I book more than one bodyguard?

Yes. For larger parties or higher-profile visits, we can arrange several bodyguards working together. Discuss your needs with us on WhatsApp.
