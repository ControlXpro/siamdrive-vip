A Morning on the Floating Markets

West of Bangkok, the canals of Ratchaburi and Samut Songkhram still carry boats laden with fruit, noodles and flowers. With a private chauffeur, you arrive early, travel at your own pace and add the extraordinary Maeklong Railway Market to the same day.

Damnoen Saduak, before the crowds

Damnoen Saduak is the best known of Thailand's floating markets, about two hours from central Bangkok. It is undeniably popular with visitors, and by late morning the main canal is crowded with tour boats. Arrive early, though, and you will see vendors paddling boats piled with mangoes, coconuts and boat noodles in soft morning light.

Your chauffeur drops you at a reputable boat pier and waits while you explore by long-tail or paddle boat. Agree the boat price and duration before you board, and do not feel obliged to buy at every stop.

Most guests find an hour on the water is plenty, followed by a slow walk along the canal-side walkways to try fresh coconut ice cream or grilled snacks.

The train through the market

At Maeklong, a busy fresh market is set directly on the railway tracks. Several times a day, a train inches through, and vendors calmly fold back their awnings and pull their produce clear before setting everything back up moments later.

Train times can change, so we plan your morning around the current schedule. Your chauffeur will make sure you arrive in good time to find a safe spot to watch, then collect you nearby afterwards.

Between trains, Maeklong is a working market where local families shop for fish, vegetables and curry pastes. It is a fascinating glimpse of everyday life, and a contrast to the more tourist-oriented canals of Damnoen Saduak.

Why a private car makes sense

Group tours follow fixed schedules and often include long shopping stops. A private chauffeur lets you decide how long to stay at each market, when to have lunch and when to return. You can visit a traditional coconut-sugar workshop, stop at a roadside fruit stall or skip anything that does not appeal.

Bangkok's outbound traffic can be heavy, so an early start matters. The return journey is relaxed, and you can rest in the car on the way back to your hotel.

Comfort counts on a day like this. Your chauffeur keeps the car cool, carries cold water and towels, and will stop whenever you need a break. Children can nap on the way home, and the generous luggage space holds any fruit, crafts or souvenirs you collect along the way.

Make it your own

On Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, Amphawa Floating Market adds a more local, Thai-style experience in the afternoon and evening. A personal assistant can translate and help you buy, and the Alphard's space is ideal for families with children.

Highlights

  • Watch the train pass through Maeklong market

Good to know

  • Agree the boat price and duration before boarding at Damnoen Saduak.
  • Wear a hat and sunscreen; there is little shade on the canals.
  • Keep well back from the tracks when the Maeklong train approaches.
  • Amphawa is livelier from mid-afternoon on Fridays to Sundays.

Boat rides are paid locally. Your chauffeur can recommend a reputable pier.
