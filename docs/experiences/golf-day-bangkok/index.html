A Bangkok Golf Day, Done Properly

Some of Asia's finest golf courses lie within easy reach of Bangkok. With a private chauffeur, you leave before dawn, arrive relaxed and return without worrying about traffic, clubs or a post-round drink.

Popular courses near Bangkok

Popular courses include Thai Country Club, east of the city off the Bang Na–Trat road and long regarded as one of the country's most polished clubs; Alpine Golf Club, north of Bangkok in Pathum Thani; and Nikanti Golf Club, west of the city in Nakhon Pathom, known for its inclusive day format and distinctive layout.

Each lies roughly an hour or so from central Bangkok, depending on traffic and the time of day. Your chauffeur plans the fastest route via the expressway network and gets you there with time for breakfast and the range.

We can help arrange tee times subject to each club's availability and policies. Some clubs have visitor restrictions or require booking through members or partners, so it is worth planning ahead.

Why start early

Thailand's heat builds steadily from late morning, and the best conditions are in the first few hours after sunrise. An early tee time means you play in cooler air and finish before the afternoon storms that often arrive in the rainy season.

Leaving Bangkok early also avoids the morning rush. Your chauffeur collects you at 5:30 am, with coffee on request, and you can rest in the car on the way.

In the cooler months from November to February, mornings can be genuinely pleasant, and a later tee time is possible. During the hot season from March to May, an early start makes a real difference.

On the course

Caddies are standard at Thai golf courses and are usually required. They know every green and are an important part of the experience; tipping your caddie is customary. Most clubs ask for collared shirts and tailored shorts or trousers, with no denim.

Your chauffeur waits at the clubhouse throughout your round, so your clubs, bags and change of clothes are always safe.

Many clubs offer excellent clubhouse facilities, from locker rooms and spas to restaurants serving Thai and international dishes. There is no rush to leave; your chauffeur will be ready whenever you are.

The right vehicle

For one or two golfers, the Toyota Camry carries two golf bags comfortably. For a four-ball, an Alphard or Vellfire offers space for players and clubs, or we can arrange two vehicles. After the round, enjoy lunch and a drink at the clubhouse before a relaxed drive back to Bangkok.

A ten-hour booking covers the full day, including travel. A personal assistant can coordinate tee times and arrangements for groups.

  • Collared shirts are required at most clubs; avoid denim.
