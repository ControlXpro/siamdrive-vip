Bangkok Experience · Chao Phraya River

ICONSIAM and the River at Dusk

As the sun drops, the Chao Phraya glows and the riverbanks come alive. This evening combines heritage, shopping and the city's most impressive riverfront mall, with your chauffeur waiting at every stop.

Begin in the old trading houses

Lhong 1919, on the Thonburi bank, is a restored Chinese trading house built around a courtyard, with a shrine to the sea goddess Mazu, murals and small shops. It is calm in the late afternoon and a lovely introduction to the river's history.

Your chauffeur drops you at the entrance and repositions to ICONSIAM, a short drive away, so the next stop is effortless.

Across the river, the old Customs House and Charoen Krung's galleries recall the era when this stretch was Bangkok's gateway to the world. The view from Lhong 1919's riverside deck takes in both banks, a fitting start to an evening that crosses between them.

ICONSIAM, inside and out

ICONSIAM is one of Asia's most ambitious shopping centres. Its ground floor houses SookSiam, an indoor floating-market concept showcasing food and crafts from Thailand's regions, while the upper levels hold international luxury houses, Thai designers and restaurants with river views.

Outside, the riverside park is the place to watch the sunset over the water. On many evenings the mall stages a multimedia fountain show along the river; times vary, so we will check the schedule for your visit.

ICONSIAM's driveways get very busy in the evening. Your chauffeur will agree a pick-up point with you, so you can leave when you choose without joining the queues.

The Gold Line, a short automated monorail, links ICONSIAM to the BTS, but at the end of an evening with shopping bags in hand most guests are glad of a car waiting at the door rather than a crowded platform.

Dinner by the water

For dinner, you could stay at ICONSIAM, cross to one of the grand hotels along Charoen Krung or board a dinner cruise. Our personal assistant service can help request reservations and cruise bookings, subject to availability.

If you take a cruise, your chauffeur will meet you at the pier where it returns, so there is no need to find transport late in the evening.

Prefer something quieter? A hotel terrace on the Thonburi bank offers views straight across to the illuminated skyline, and your chauffeur can take you there directly from ICONSIAM.

A nightcap at Asiatique

End the evening at Asiatique The Riverfront, a night market in restored warehouses with a large Ferris wheel. It is lively until late, and when you are ready to leave, your car is waiting at the entrance. A five-hour booking covers most of this evening, and extra hours can be added if you choose to linger.

Highlights

  • Heritage courtyards at Lhong 1919
  • SookSiam's regional food and crafts
  • Sunset from ICONSIAM's riverside park
  • Pier pick-ups after dinner cruises
  • Late-evening Ferris wheel at Asiatique

Good to know

  • ICONSIAM's driveway is busy after 6 pm; agree a side entrance for pick-up.
  • Check the fountain show schedule before you plan dinner.
  • Bring a light layer; air-conditioning in malls is strong.
  • Dinner cruises book up at weekends; plan ahead if you want one.
