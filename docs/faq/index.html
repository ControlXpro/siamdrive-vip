Help centre

Questions, answered.

Can't find it here? Ask the concierge on WhatsApp.

Booking

How do I book with SiamDrive?

All bookings are made on WhatsApp at +66 96 221 2364. Send us your date, pickup address, destination or schedule, number of passengers and luggage, and we will reply with the price for your chosen vehicle.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for airport arrivals, weddings, events and our limited-edition vehicles. Shorter-notice requests are welcome too; message us and we will confirm availability as quickly as possible.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your booking is confirmed once you have agreed the details and price on WhatsApp and payment has been arranged. You will receive written confirmation in the same conversation.

Can I book on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Assistants, travel managers and family members often book for guests. Just give us the passenger's name, contact number and travel details.

Can I change my booking after it is confirmed?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the change — a new time, an extra stop, a different flight — and we will confirm whether it affects the arrangements or the price.

Pricing & Payment

What is included in the price?

All chauffeur prices include the driver, fuel and tolls. The only exception is monthly private driver packages, where fuel and tolls are excluded and settled as used.

Are your prices fixed?

Yes. Transfers, hourly hire and day trips are priced per vehicle from our rate table. The price you confirm on WhatsApp is the price you pay for the agreed service.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer to our Kasikornbank account. Payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp or on your invoice when you book.

What happens if my booking runs over time?

Additional hours are charged at the overtime rate shown in our rate table for your vehicle or service. Simply tell your chauffeur or message us if you need more time.

Can you invoice my company?

Yes. We issue invoices to companies and organisations, which is common for corporate travel and delegations. Payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Airport & Fast-Track

Which airports do you serve?

We provide transfers from and to Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Don Mueang (DMK) and U-Tapao (UTP), including direct transfers between Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang.

Where will my chauffeur meet me?

Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall after you have collected your luggage. If you have booked fast-track at Suvarnabhumi, the fast-track team walks you through the terminal and hands you over to your chauffeur.

Can I book fast-track on its own?

No. Fast-track and the VIP electric buggy at Suvarnabhumi are only available together with a SiamDrive car booking.

How many people can ride in a VIP buggy?

Each buggy seats a maximum of two guests. Larger parties book one buggy for every two travellers, and children under three ride free.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book and your pickup will follow your actual arrival time. For major changes, such as a new flight, message us on WhatsApp and we will rearrange.

Bodyguards & Security

Who are your bodyguards?

Our bodyguards are suited, English-speaking, discreet security professionals. They accompany you in the car and on foot, staying alert without drawing attention.

Can I book a bodyguard without a car?

No. Bodyguards are only bookable together with a SiamDrive car booking, so your chauffeur and bodyguard work from the same plan.

How long can I book a bodyguard for?

For a single transfer, or for five or ten hours. Additional hours are charged at the overtime rate, and a supplement applies for assignments outside Bangkok.

What is a motorcycle escort?

Motorcycle escort riders travel with your convoy, clearing the way ahead and keeping multiple vehicles together through Bangkok traffic. They are escort riders, not police.

Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. Details of your schedule, venues and party are shared only with the chauffeurs and security staff assigned to your booking.

Vehicles

Which vehicles do you offer?

Our fleet includes the Toyota Camry, Toyota Alphard 30, Toyota Alphard 40, Rowen Vellfire Z, Toyota Alphard 40 Executive Lounge and Porsche Cayenne S.

How many passengers and bags fit in each car?

The Camry and Cayenne S seat up to three passengers with two large suitcases. The Alphard, Vellfire and Alphard Executive seat up to five with four large suitcases.

Which car should I choose for a family?

The Alphard 30, Alphard 40 or Vellfire Z. Their sliding doors, captain's chairs and luggage space make them ideal for families arriving with children and suitcases.

Is every vehicle driven by a chauffeur?

Yes. SiamDrive is a chauffeur service; all vehicles are driven by our professional drivers. Self-drive hire is not available.

What if my group does not fit in one vehicle?

We can provide several vehicles travelling together, coordinated through one booking. For larger convoys, motorcycle escort riders can keep everyone together.

Policies

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on the service and are confirmed with you at the time of booking, so you know exactly where you stand before you pay.

What is the scope of the personal assistant service?

Our personal assistants provide strictly professional companionship and assistance: translation, errands, shopping, sightseeing support and nanny care. The service is booked as ten hours in Bangkok.

Are fuel and tolls included in monthly packages?

No. Monthly packages include a private driver seven days a week, ten hours a day, with unlimited kilometres, but exclude fuel and tolls. How these are settled is agreed when you book.

What happens if I leave something in the car?

Message us on WhatsApp straight away. Your chauffeur will check the vehicle and we will arrange to return any items found.

Is there an extra charge for overnight trips?

When a chauffeur stays overnight away from Bangkok with you, an overnight supplement covers their accommodation. It is included in your quote before you confirm.
