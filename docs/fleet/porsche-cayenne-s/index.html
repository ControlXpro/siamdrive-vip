Performance SUV · 2026+ · Up to 3 guests

When the Journey Is the Statement

The Porsche Cayenne S brings sports-car engineering to a luxury SUV. With its red-leather cabin and unmistakable presence, it is the choice when you want your arrival — and every moment of the drive — to feel exceptional.

3+1 seats2 large bagsPerformance SUVAirport 19,000 THB

Cabin and comfort

Open the door to a cabin finished in rich red leather, with supportive seats and the precise, high-quality detailing Porsche is known for. The ride is composed and confident, whether gliding through Sukhumvit traffic or cruising on the motorway towards the coast. Even a short hop across the city feels like an occasion.

Rear passengers enjoy comfortable seating and their own air-conditioning vents, while the chauffeur makes the most of a powerful twin-turbocharged engine with smooth, measured driving rather than theatrics. Ride quality is firm but refined, in the way of a well-engineered performance car, and the cabin remains calm at motorway speeds.

Best uses for the Cayenne S

The Cayenne S is for occasions that call for presence. Couples book it as a wedding car; executives choose it to arrive at a launch or gala with quiet authority; travellers reserve it for a birthday, an anniversary or a memorable night out in Bangkok. It is never brash; it simply makes it clear that the occasion matters.

It also suits a stylish airport collection for one or two guests, or a day trip where the drive itself is part of the enjoyment. Pair it with a suited bodyguard for high-profile appearances, or with fast-track at Suvarnabhumi for a seamless arrival.

Because the Cayenne S is a premium performance vehicle, its rates sit at the top of our rate table. Most clients book it for a specific moment — the arrival, the celebration, the evening — rather than for routine transport, and plan the rest of the trip around an Alphard or Camry.

Luggage and passengers

The Cayenne S seats up to three passengers, with space for two large suitcases. Two people travel most comfortably on longer journeys, while three fit well for evening outings and short transfers. Hand luggage and shopping bags fit easily alongside.

If you are travelling with more luggage, consider pairing the Cayenne with a second vehicle for bags, or choose an Alphard or Vellfire for the main journey and the Cayenne for the moments that matter.

How many people fit in the Cayenne S?

Up to three passengers plus the chauffeur, with space for two large suitcases.

Is the Cayenne S driven by a chauffeur?

Yes. Like all SiamDrive vehicles, it is always driven by a professional chauffeur. Self-drive hire is not available.

Can I book the Cayenne S for a wedding?

Yes, it is a popular wedding car. We recommend booking early for popular dates.

Can I use the Cayenne S for long transfers?

Yes. It is comfortable on the motorway and available for transfers and day trips, including Pattaya and Hua Hin.
