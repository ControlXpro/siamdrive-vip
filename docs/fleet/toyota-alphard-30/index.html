Luxury MPV · 2020 · Up to 5 guests

Bangkok's Favourite VIP Van

There is a reason the Alphard became the vehicle of choice for VIP travel across Asia. The Alphard 30 combines a lounge-like cabin with space for families and small groups, making it the most versatile car in our fleet.

5+1 seats4 large bagsLuxury MPVAirport 6,000 THB

Cabin and comfort

Power sliding doors open onto a tall, airy cabin where you can step in rather than climb down. The second row has individual captain's chairs, typically with leg rests, giving the two main passengers the feeling of a business-class seat rather than the back of a car.

Rear climate controls, reading lights and sunshades let passengers set their own environment, and the high seating position gives an excellent view of the city as you travel. For a family after a long flight, it is the difference between arriving tired and arriving rested: children can nap, adults can stretch their legs, and nobody has a suitcase on their lap.

The 2020 model year means a mature, proven vehicle rather than the very latest design, but the essentials that matter on the road — space, quiet and comfortable seating — are all there.

Where the Alphard 30 shines

The Alphard 30 is the all-rounder. Families use it for airport arrivals, when children, grandparents and luggage all need to travel together. Business teams use it for client visits, where three or four colleagues can talk face to face on the way. Groups of friends choose it for nights out and shopping days.

It is also one of the two vehicles offered on our monthly private driver packages, alongside the Alphard 40. For clients who want Alphard space and comfort at a more accessible rate, it is the sensible choice.

Luggage and groups

The Alphard 30 seats up to five passengers and takes four large suitcases, which covers most family holidays and small business groups. For long-haul travellers with more luggage than usual, or for groups carrying golf bags, mention it at booking and we will suggest whether a second vehicle would be more comfortable.

The sliding doors and low step are especially helpful for older passengers and anyone travelling with young children. If you are travelling with young children, tell us their ages when you book so we can discuss seating arrangements with you in advance.

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

What is the difference between the Alphard 30 and Alphard 40?

The Alphard 40 is the newer generation, with an updated cabin and blacked-out finish. The Alphard 30 offers the same practical layout and space at a lower rate.

How many people fit in the Alphard 30?

Up to five passengers, plus the chauffeur, with space for four large suitcases.

Is the Alphard 30 available monthly?

Yes. It is one of the two vehicles offered on our monthly packages, which include a private driver seven days a week, ten hours a day, with unlimited kilometres.

Is it suitable for older passengers?

Very. The power sliding doors, low step and supportive captain's chairs make getting in and out easy.
