Executive Lounge MPV · 2025+ · Flagship

A First-Class Cabin on Wheels

The Alphard 40 Executive Lounge is the flagship of our fleet. It takes the new-generation Alphard and reimagines the rear cabin as a private lounge, with reclining ottoman seats designed for passengers who want to work, rest or simply arrive refreshed.

5+1 seats4 large bagsExecutive Lounge MPVAirport 11,000 THB

Cabin and comfort

The heart of the Executive Lounge is its pair of second-row seats. Larger and more generously cushioned than the standard Alphard's, they recline deeply with ottoman leg rests, so you can stretch out after a long-haul flight or settle in for a journey to the coast. Fold-out tables, where fitted, give you a surface for a laptop or a coffee.

Rear passengers have their own controls for climate, lighting and seat position, typically through a dedicated rear-cabin panel, and the cabin is finished to a noticeably higher standard. Privacy sunshades and a hushed ride complete the sense of travelling in a first-class cabin.

Best uses for the Executive Lounge

This is the vehicle for principals: chief executives, senior officials, public figures and guests for whom comfort and privacy are non-negotiable. It is especially suited to airport arrivals after long flights, when a reclined seat and a quiet cabin make all the difference.

It is also an exceptional choice for weddings, milestone celebrations and long transfers to Hua Hin or Khao Yai, where the journey itself becomes part of the experience. Pair it with fast-track at Suvarnabhumi and a suited bodyguard for a complete VIP arrival.

Because it is our flagship, we recommend reserving it as early as possible for important dates, and particularly for multi-day visits where you would like the same car and chauffeur throughout.

Luggage and passengers

The Executive Lounge is designed around its two principal seats, though the vehicle can carry up to five passengers in total, with room for four large suitcases. For a principal travelling with a larger entourage or significant luggage, we often recommend a second vehicle so the lounge seats remain uncrowded. Many principals prefer to travel with just one companion, leaving the second lounge seat free for documents, a jacket or simply space.

Tell us who is travelling and what they are carrying, and we will plan the arrangement accordingly. Your chauffeur handles every bag and door, so the principal passenger steps straight from the kerb into the lounge seat.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

What makes the Executive Lounge different from the standard Alphard 40?

The rear cabin is upgraded with larger, deeply reclining lounge seats, ottoman leg rests, dedicated rear controls and a higher standard of finish.

How many passengers can travel in it?

Up to five, though it is designed around its two principal seats. For larger parties, a second vehicle keeps the lounge comfortable.

Can I combine it with fast-track and a bodyguard?

Yes. At Suvarnabhumi, fast-track and the VIP buggy can be added, and a suited, English-speaking bodyguard can travel with you.

Is it suitable for working on the move?

Very. The quiet cabin, adjustable seating and rear controls make it easy to take calls, review documents or rest between meetings.
