FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

How does hiring a private driver work?

Choose a vehicle and book a 5-hour or 10-hour block. Your chauffeur collects you and stays with the car for the whole booking — meetings, shopping, dinners, as many stops as you like. Extra time is billed per hour at the published overtime rate.

Can I add bodyguards to my driver booking?

Yes. Suited, English-speaking bodyguards are booked per guard for a transfer, 5 hours or 10 hours, and are coordinated with your chauffeur so car and protection move as one. Protection is always booked together with a SiamDrive vehicle.

Are fuel, tolls and the driver included?

Yes. Every chauffeured rate includes the driver, fuel and expressway tolls. Monthly packages exclude fuel and tolls. Overtime, overnight stays and add-ons are listed upfront.

Can I keep the same driver for my whole stay?

Monthly packages give you a dedicated chauffeur and car seven days a week, ten hours a day, with unlimited kilometres. For shorter stays, ask the concierge to keep the same chauffeur across your bookings.

How quickly can you confirm?

Send your booking from the form or message us on WhatsApp — the concierge confirms vehicle, chauffeur and fixed price, usually within minutes, 24 hours a day.

Do you also do airport pick-ups?

Of course. We meet you in arrivals with a name sign and track your flight; at Suvarnabhumi you can add Fast-Track and a private electric buggy from the aircraft door.
