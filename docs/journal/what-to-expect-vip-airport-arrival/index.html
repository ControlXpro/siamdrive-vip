What to Expect From a VIP Arrival in Bangkok

From the moment you book to the moment you reach your hotel: a calm walk-through of a VIP arrival in Bangkok, and the small details that make it seamless.

Before you fly

A good arrival begins days before the aircraft lands. Once the booking is confirmed, the ground team needs a short list of essentials: passenger names as they appear in passports, the flight number and date, a contact number that will work on landing, the number of bags and any particular requirements, from a wheelchair to a child seat. With these, a greeter, a buggy where available and the right vehicle can be allocated.

Choosing the vehicle is part of this stage. A couple travelling light will be comfortable in an executive sedan, while a family, a group or anyone with golf bags or several large cases needs the space of a luxury MPV. If in doubt, err on the side of more room; jet lag and cramped luggage are a poor combination.

Check your entry requirements before travelling, too. Thailand asks foreign visitors to complete the Thailand Digital Arrival Card online ahead of arrival, and visa rules vary by nationality. A VIP service smooths the process but cannot replace the paperwork, so it pays to have documents in order.

On landing

Your flight is tracked, so early and late arrivals are absorbed without any need for you to send updates. When you switch your phone back on, a short message is usually waiting to confirm that the greeter is in position.

As you leave the aircraft, the greeter meets you at the gate with a name board. At Suvarnabhumi, where the walk to immigration can be long, an electric buggy may carry you through the concourse. You are then escorted to a priority immigration lane, and on through baggage reclaim and customs, with a porter for heavy luggage if needed. Waiting for checked bags is the one part no service can shorten, though having someone to watch the belt while you sit down makes it considerably more bearable.

Meeting your chauffeur

As you clear customs, the greeter lets your chauffeur know, so the car is brought to the arrivals kerb as you walk out rather than parked several levels away. Luggage is loaded for you, and you step into a cabin that has been cooled in advance — a welcome contrast with the humid air outside, whatever the hour.

The drive into central Bangkok typically takes thirty minutes to an hour, depending on traffic and the time of day. A good chauffeur reads the mood: some arrivals want quiet and a dimmed cabin after a long flight, others want a brief orientation to the city, a recommendation for dinner or a stop at a pharmacy on the way. Neither is wrong; simply say which you prefer.

Don Mueang and U-Tapao

Not every arrival is at Suvarnabhumi. Don Mueang, to the north of the city, handles mostly low-cost and domestic flights. It is more compact, with fewer premium facilities, so a greeter’s main role is to guide you efficiently through busy halls and straight to the car.

U-Tapao, around 175 kilometres south-east of Bangkok near Pattaya and Rayong, handles a mix of charter, regional and domestic flights. It is quieter, and for travellers heading to the Eastern Seaboard it can be far more convenient than flying into Bangkok. The drive to Bangkok typically takes around two and a half hours, while Pattaya is much closer.

Small details that make a difference

The most memorable arrivals are usually the ones where nothing needed to be explained twice. A few details shared at the time of booking help the team prepare.

None of this needs to be formal. A few lines in a message are enough, and anything forgotten can be added later. The point is simply that the people meeting you know who you are, what you need and where you are going, so that the first hour in Bangkok feels effortless rather than improvised.

  • Your first destination: a hotel, a residence, an office or a restaurant straight from the airport.
  • Luggage, including golf bags, pushchairs and oversized cases.
  • Any mobility needs or medical considerations.
  • Whether you need a local SIM card or eSIM, or currency, on arrival.
  • Preferences in the car: temperature, quiet or conversation, a phone charger for your device.

Leaving as smoothly as you arrived

For travellers returning regularly, these preferences tend to be remembered, and later arrivals become even simpler. The greeter recognises the face, the chauffeur knows the address, and the whole process takes on the ease of coming home rather than arriving somewhere new.

The same care applies on departure: a pick-up time planned around realistic traffic, a greeter at the kerb, help at check-in and a priority lane through passport control. FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Where exactly will I meet my greeter?

In most cases at the aircraft gate, as you leave the aerobridge, holding a name board. Exact arrangements depend on the airport and gate, and are confirmed before you fly.

What if immigration or baggage takes longer than expected?

The greeter stays with you throughout, and the chauffeur is kept informed, so the car is ready when you emerge regardless of how long the formalities take.

Can a VIP arrival be arranged at U-Tapao Airport?

Yes, arrivals can be arranged at U-Tapao as well as Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang. U-Tapao is particularly convenient for Pattaya, Rayong and the Eastern Seaboard.
