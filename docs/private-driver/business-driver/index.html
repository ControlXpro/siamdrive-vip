Between Meetings, Never Between Cars

The hours between meetings are where a business day in Bangkok is won or lost. A private business driver keeps one car and one discreet chauffeur with you all day, so you arrive composed, on time and ready to work.

5 hrs from 8,000 THB10 hrs from 12,000 THBFuel & tolls included

Built for a schedule that moves

Book your driver for five or ten hours and work through the day exactly as it unfolds. A meeting in Sathorn overruns, a lunch moves to Thonglor, a client asks you to stop by their office in Asok; your chauffeur simply adjusts the route.

There is no rebooking between appointments and no waiting on a hot pavement for a ride that may or may not arrive. While you are inside, the car waits nearby, cool and ready, with your documents and laptop safely on the seat.

If you are joining a call from the car, tell your chauffeur; they will favour calmer roads and avoid the stop-start of the busiest junctions so you can concentrate.

Roadshows and multi-stop days

Investor roadshows, supplier visits and sales days often mean six or seven stops across Silom, Sukhumvit, Rama IX and Bang Na. Your chauffeur plans the sequence with the traffic in mind and knows that the office tower's front entrance is not always the fastest place to be collected.

Many Bangkok towers have separate drop-off bays, basement car parks and security desks. Knowing which door to use, and arriving early enough to clear reception, is the quiet detail that keeps a tight programme intact.

Your chauffeur waits until you are inside before moving the car, and when you leave they are back at the right door rather than out on the main road, which matters when the next meeting is across the river.

A cabin to work in

Bangkok journeys can be long, so the car becomes part of your office. Take calls, review a presentation or prepare for the next room in peace. The Toyota Camry is understated and efficient for one or two executives; an Alphard or the Rowen Vellfire Z suits a team travelling together; and the Alphard 40 Executive Lounge gives a senior guest reclining seats and genuine privacy.

Discretion is standard. Your chauffeur is courteous, punctual and unobtrusive, and conversations in the car stay in the car.

For a visiting guest arriving straight from a long flight, preparation for the first meeting can begin in the car itself, with a quiet cabin and space to spread out notes.

Booking for executives and visitors

Executive assistants and travel managers can book on a guest's behalf. Send the passenger name, schedule and meeting addresses on WhatsApp, and we confirm the vehicle and price in writing; changes are handled in the same thread, and our English-speaking concierge is available around the clock.

Driver, fuel and tolls are included in every block, and overtime is billed by the hour if the day runs long. For sensitive visits, a suited bodyguard can be added to the same booking. For assignments of several weeks, a monthly package may suit better.

The same thread keeps a clear written record of every pickup, vehicle and timing, which is especially useful when several colleagues arrive on different days or a programme changes overnight.

A typical day

  1. 08:00

    Hotel pickup

    Collected early to reach Silom ahead of the worst of the morning traffic, with the day's addresses already in hand.

  2. 09:00

    First meeting, Silom

    Set down at the tower's drop-off bay; your driver waits in the basement car park.

  3. 11:30

    Client office, Rama IX

    A cross-town move used for a call and a final look at the slides.

  4. 13:00

    Working lunch, Sukhumvit

    Your chauffeur repositions while the lunch runs long and is at the door when you step out.

  5. 16:00

    Site visit, Bang Na

    A final appointment on the eastern side of the city, then back to the hotel as the evening peak eases.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Can someone book on my behalf?

Yes. Most business bookings are made by assistants or travel managers. Send the passenger's name, contact number, schedule and addresses, and we confirm everything in writing.

Which car is best for a senior executive?

The Alphard 40 Executive Lounge, with reclining seats and a private cabin, is the usual choice. For a lower-key arrival, the Toyota Camry is discreet and efficient.

What happens if a meeting overruns?

Your chauffeur waits. If the whole day runs past your booked block, extra time is billed hourly at the overtime rate, subject to availability.

Can we use several cars for a visiting team?

Yes. We can coordinate several vehicles from a single booking so the team moves together through the day.

Can I add security for a sensitive visit?

A suited, English-speaking bodyguard can be added to any driver booking and briefed on your schedule in advance.
