Bangkok, Without the Language Barrier

Few things are more tiring in a new city than trying to explain where you want to go. With an English-speaking private driver and a concierge who answers in English around the clock, your Bangkok day runs on plain conversation rather than pointing at a map.

No more lost-in-translation journeys

Bangkok addresses are notoriously tricky. Hotels and restaurants often have different Thai and English names, many venues sit deep down a numbered soi, and a single wrong turn on a one-way street can add twenty minutes. Taxi drivers frequently work only from the Thai name.

Your chauffeur speaks working English for directions and the practicalities of the day. Tell them the next stop in your own words, ask how long the drive will take or where to meet afterwards, and get a clear answer.

That clarity matters most at the moments that usually go wrong: being dropped at the wrong entrance of a vast mall, agreeing where to be collected after a show, or explaining that you need a cash machine on the way.

A concierge in English, day and night

Behind every driver is our English-speaking concierge, available 24 hours a day. Your booking, changes and any questions are handled in writing, in English, so there is always a clear record of the plan.

If something needs more detailed explanation in Thai, such as a restaurant's directions, a hotel's car-park rules or a change of pickup point, the concierge relays it to your chauffeur so nothing is lost between languages.

You can write to us in English before you arrive, too, to ask which car suits your party, how long a journey takes at a particular hour or whether your plan for the day is realistic.

Local knowledge you can actually use

A driver you can talk to is also a source of practical local knowledge. Ask which malls are quieter on a weekday, when the traffic towards the river will ease, or whether a market is worth visiting in the midday heat. Your chauffeur will tell you honestly.

Our drivers are chauffeurs rather than licensed tour guides, so for in-depth history at the Grand Palace or Ayutthaya we suggest a local guide on site. For interpreting at meetings, banks or medical appointments, a personal assistant can join your booking.

Small things become easy when you can simply ask: where the nearest clean restroom is, which side of the road to wait on, or whether it is worth leaving now to beat an afternoon downpour.

How the booking works

Hire your English-speaking driver for five or ten hours within Bangkok, with driver, fuel and tolls included and overtime billed by the hour. The car stays with you throughout, waiting outside each stop with the air-conditioning on.

Choose the vehicle that suits you, from the discreet Toyota Camry to an Alphard for families and groups, the Alphard 40 Executive Lounge for long working days or the Porsche Cayenne S for an evening with presence. If good English matters especially for your day, mention it when booking and we will match the driver accordingly.

Once your plans are confirmed, you will have everything in writing, in English, before your driver arrives.
