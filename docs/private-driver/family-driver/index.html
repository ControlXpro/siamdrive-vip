Bangkok with Children, Made Easy

Children, heat and Bangkok traffic can turn a simple day out into hard work. A private family driver keeps a cool, spacious car with you from breakfast to bedtime, ready at every mall, aquarium and restaurant door.

5 hrs from 8,000 THB10 hrs from 12,000 THBFuel & tolls included

Space for everyone and everything

Families usually choose an Alphard or the Rowen Vellfire Z. Wide sliding doors make it easy to lift a sleepy toddler in and out, the captain's chairs give older children their own seat, and there is room behind for a folded pushchair, a nappy bag and the day's shopping.

Child seats are available on request; ask when booking and tell us the children's ages so we can confirm what is possible. If your party is larger, two cars are often more comfortable than one crowded one.

Tell us how much luggage you are bringing as well as how many people. Bangkok shopping has a way of filling a boot by mid-afternoon, and it is better to have the space than to need it.

Days out without the waiting

Bangkok has plenty for children: the aquarium and the big malls around Siam, the riverside at ICONSIAM, parks, play centres and, at weekends, the bustle of Chatuchak. What it lacks is easy transport between them for a family carrying bags in the midday heat.

With your own driver, you walk out of each stop into a car that has been waiting with the air-conditioning on. No standing at a taxi rank with tired children, no folding a pushchair into a small boot, no explaining the next address.

Mall car parks fill early at weekends and during school holidays. Your chauffeur deals with the queue for the drop-off lane and the search for a space while you are already inside and the children are already pressed against the glass.

Pacing the day for small travellers

Children set the rhythm, so the plan should bend. A nap in the car between stops is often the best rest of the day, and a calm chauffeur will take the smoother route rather than the quickest one when someone is asleep.

Tell us your children's routines when you book and we will suggest a sensible order: outdoor sights early before the heat, indoor attractions in the afternoon, and home before the evening traffic builds. Your driver can also wait while one parent takes the children back to the hotel and the other carries on.

Little things help too: a spare bottle of water, a bag of snacks left in the car, a change of clothes for the inevitable spill. Everything stays where you left it until you need it.

Five hours, ten hours or longer

A five-hour block suits a morning outing; ten hours covers a full family day. Driver, fuel and tolls are included, and extra time is billed by the hour if the day runs on. For families settling into Bangkok, a monthly package with an Alphard and a dedicated driver covers school runs, weekend trips and errands seven days a week.

Want another pair of hands? A personal assistant can join as a nanny or companion, and a suited bodyguard can be added for extra peace of mind in crowded places.

Our English-speaking concierge is available around the clock if plans change in the middle of the day.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Do you provide child seats?

Child seats are available on request. Ask when booking and tell us your children's ages so we can confirm what is possible for your date.

Which vehicle is best for a family?

An Alphard or the Rowen Vellfire Z, which seat up to five guests with room for four large suitcases. Larger families often book two vehicles.

Can we leave things in the car?

Yes. Pushchairs, shopping and spare clothes stay safely with your driver between stops.

Can the driver take part of the family back to the hotel?

Yes, within your booked hours. Your chauffeur can drop some of the family at the hotel and return for the others.

How do we book?

Send us your date, hours, pickup address, the number and ages of children and any luggage on WhatsApp. We confirm the vehicle and price and send payment details.
