Five Hours, Entirely at Your Disposal

A morning of appointments, an afternoon of shopping or an evening out that deserves better than a taxi rank. Book a private driver for five hours and your car follows your plan, stop by stop, with your chauffeur waiting outside every door.

The right length for most Bangkok plans

Five hours is the block most guests reach for first. It covers a working morning across Sathorn and Silom, a long lunch and a fitting in Siam, or a relaxed evening of dinner and a rooftop bar, without paying for a full day you will not use.

Your time begins at the agreed pickup at your address. From then on the car and chauffeur are yours: go where you like, in the order you like, and change your mind as often as the day requires. The number of stops makes no difference to the price.

Half-day hire also suits arrival day. Land in the morning, rest for a few hours, then spend a late afternoon getting your bearings with a driver who can show you where things are, how long journeys really take and which neighbourhoods are worth returning to.

Planning around the traffic

Bangkok traffic follows a rhythm. Mornings build from around seven, the late afternoon is heavier still, and Friday evenings can turn a short hop into a long crawl. A half-day block works best when it sits between those peaks, roughly late morning to mid-afternoon, or after the evening rush has eased.

Your chauffeur knows which sois run one way, where a U-turn will cost ten minutes and which hotel forecourts allow a car to wait. Tell them your next stop and let them choose the approach; a calm route usually beats a short one.

If you need to be somewhere at a fixed time, say so when you book. Your chauffeur will build in a margin for the traffic, so a slow stretch on Rama IV does not become a missed appointment.

The car stays with you

This is the real difference from ride-hailing. While you are inside a meeting, a mall or a restaurant, your driver stays close with the engine running and the air-conditioning on. Shopping bags, a laptop or a jacket can stay on the seat.

When you are ready to leave, a short message brings the car back to the entrance. In the midday heat, stepping straight from a lobby into a cool cabin is a luxury you notice within the first hour.

It also means you never have to step into the road to flag anything down. Your chauffeur handles the set-down, opens the door and deals with the attendants at busy mall entrances, so each arrival takes seconds rather than minutes.

When five hours becomes six

Plans rarely end exactly on time. If your afternoon runs on, simply tell your chauffeur; additional time is billed by the hour at the overtime rate for your vehicle, subject to the driver's availability. If you suspect the day will stretch well beyond five hours, a ten-hour block is usually the better choice from the outset.

Choose the car to suit the occasion: the Toyota Camry for a discreet solo or couple's outing, an Alphard or the Rowen Vellfire Z for a family or small group, the Alphard 40 Executive Lounge when you want to rest between stops, or the Porsche Cayenne S when the arrival matters.

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

When do my five hours start?

At the agreed pickup time at your address. You are not charged for the time it takes your driver to reach you.

What if I need the car for longer?

Tell your chauffeur or our concierge. Extra time is billed hourly at your vehicle's overtime rate, subject to the driver's availability.

Are fuel and tolls really included?

Yes. Driver, fuel and expressway tolls are all included in a five-hour booking within Bangkok, so the only variable is time.

Can I book a half-day for an evening?

Of course. Many guests book five hours from early evening for dinner, a show or a rooftop bar, with the car waiting until they are ready to go home.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with the date, start time, pickup address and preferred vehicle. We confirm the price and send payment details, and your booking is secured once paid.
