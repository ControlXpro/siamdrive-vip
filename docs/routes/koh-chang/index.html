Bangkok to Koh Chang, unhurried

Koh Chang is Thailand's third-largest island, a mountain of rainforest rising from the sea near the Cambodian border. It is a long way from Bangkok, which is precisely why the journey deserves a comfortable private car and a chauffeur who knows the road to Trat.

≈ 315 km4.5–5 hrsEastern IslandsQuote on request

Two stages: road, then ferry

Your chauffeur drives you approximately 315 km from Bangkok to the Laem Ngop area in Trat province, where the ferries to Koh Chang depart. The route typically follows Motorway 7 towards Chonburi, then Highway 36 and Sukhumvit Road, Highway 3, through Rayong and Chanthaburi. Allow around four and a half to five hours, including a rest stop.

At the coast, the car drops you at the ferry pier your plans require, usually Ao Thammachat or Centrepoint, both close to Laem Ngop. Vehicle ferries cross to the island in roughly 30 to 45 minutes, and you travel on board as a foot passenger with your luggage.

Arriving on the island

Koh Chang's ferry piers are on the north-east coast, while most resorts line the west coast along White Sand Beach, Klong Prao, Kai Bae and Lonely Beach. Many resorts arrange a pick-up from the island pier; if yours does, share the details with us and we will time your departure from Bangkok so you meet the ferry with a comfortable margin.

The island rewards slow travel. Klong Plu waterfall is an easy walk through the forest, Bang Bao in the south is a fishing village built on stilts over the water, and boat trips to the smaller islands of the archipelago leave from the southern coast.

Island roads are steep and winding between the beaches, so we recommend settling into your resort before exploring and using local taxis or your hotel's driver once there.

Why go by private chauffeur

The alternatives are a long bus ride with a change in Trat, or a flight to Trat airport followed by a transfer. A private car keeps you on your own schedule, with legroom for five hours, a stop for lunch whenever you like and no luggage handling between vehicles until the ferry.

Families and groups will find an Alphard or Vellfire especially welcome on a journey of this length. Your chauffeur can also break the trip in Chanthaburi for its riverside quarter or cathedral, turning the transfer into a gentle half-day of sightseeing.

Pricing on request

Because the distance and pier arrangements vary, Koh Chang transfers are quoted individually. Send us your date, pick-up address, group size and ferry or resort details on WhatsApp, and we will confirm a fixed price with fuel, tolls and the chauffeur included before you commit.

We can also arrange the return leg from the mainland pier, so the car is waiting when your ferry docks at the end of your stay.

Chauffeur's tips

  • Leave Bangkok by 7am to reach the pier around midday and the island by early afternoon.
  • Ferries run less often in the evening, so avoid planning to reach the pier after late afternoon.
  • From around June to October the west coast can have rougher seas; check your resort's beach conditions.
  • Keep a light jacket and sea-sickness tablets handy for the ferry crossing.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Does the car cross to Koh Chang?

Your chauffeur drops you at the mainland ferry pier near Laem Ngop, and you cross as a foot passenger. If you need a car on the island, ask on WhatsApp and we will advise.

How long does the whole journey take?

Around four and a half to five hours by road for approximately 315 km, plus roughly 30 to 45 minutes on the ferry and the drive to your resort.

Why is the price on request?

Koh Chang is a long-distance route with varying pier and timing requirements, so we confirm a fixed price on WhatsApp for your exact plan.

Is the ferry ticket included?

No. Our price covers the road transfer, fuel, tolls and chauffeur. Ferry tickets are bought at the pier.
