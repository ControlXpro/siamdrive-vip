Bangkok to Koh Samet, pier to paradise

Koh Samet is the closest island escape to Bangkok with genuinely white sand and clear water. We drive you to Ban Phe Pier on the Rayong coast, timed for your ferry or speedboat, so the only queue you meet is the one for the boat.

≈ 200 km3 hrsEastern SeaboardFrom 16,000 THB

How the journey works

Cars do not travel to Koh Samet, so the transfer has two stages. Your chauffeur drives you approximately 200 km from Bangkok to Ban Phe Pier, usually via Motorway 7 and Highway 36, in around three hours. At the pier, he helps you with luggage to the ticket counter or straight to your boat.

From Ban Phe, regular passenger ferries cross to Na Dan Pier on the island in roughly 40 minutes, while speedboats take around 15 to 20 minutes and can drop you at the beach in front of many resorts. Several piers operate along the Ban Phe waterfront, so tell us which one your resort uses.

Choosing ferry or speedboat

Ferries are inexpensive and run frequently during the day, but schedules thin out in the late afternoon. Speedboats cost more and can be chartered privately, which suits families with a lot of luggage or anyone arriving after the last regular boat.

Many island resorts will arrange a speedboat transfer for you. If yours does, send us the boat time on WhatsApp and we will plan your departure from Bangkok so you arrive with a comfortable margin rather than a rush.

The island itself

Koh Samet is part of a national park, and an entry fee is collected on arrival. Sai Kaew Beach is the liveliest, Ao Wong Duean is a sheltered crescent, and Ao Prao on the quieter west coast is known for sunsets and some of the island's more refined resorts.

Inland, the roads are rough and transport is by songthaew or rented scooter, which is why we recommend travelling light and letting the boat do the heavy lifting.

The return

We can meet your boat at Ban Phe on the way back, with the car waiting near the pier. Koh Samet is priced on our long-distance tier, with fuel, tolls and the driver included; see the rate table below.

Because sea conditions can delay boats, your chauffeur keeps an eye on messages rather than a fixed clock. If your speedboat is running late, simply let us know on WhatsApp and the car will still be there when you step onto the pier.

Many guests also use the return drive for a final seafood lunch in Ban Phe or a stop at a Rayong fruit stall before reaching Bangkok in the early evening, rested and without having handled a single suitcase in a crowded bus station.

Chauffeur's tips

  • Aim to reach Ban Phe by early afternoon; regular ferry departures become less frequent later in the day.
  • Pack a small day bag for the boat and keep valuables in a dry pouch, as speedboats can be splashy.
  • Carry some cash for the national park fee and island songthaews, which rarely take cards.
  • On holiday weekends, book speedboats in advance through your resort.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Does the car go onto Koh Samet?

No. Your chauffeur drops you at Ban Phe Pier on the mainland, and you continue by ferry or speedboat to the island.

How long is the boat crossing?

Roughly 40 minutes by ferry to Na Dan Pier, or around 15 to 20 minutes by speedboat, depending on the sea and your beach.

Can you pick us up when we come back?

Yes. Send your return boat time on WhatsApp and the car will be waiting at Ban Phe Pier.

Is the boat ticket included?

No. Our price covers the road transfer, fuel, tolls and chauffeur. Boat tickets are bought at the pier or through your resort.
