Suvarnabhumi to Hua Hin, door to door

Hua Hin lies on the far side of Bangkok from Suvarnabhumi, which makes a well-planned route essential. Your chauffeur skirts the southern edge of the city and heads down the Royal Coast, so you go from aircraft to beach resort in one smooth journey.

BKK arrivals≈ 230 km3–3.5 hrsFrom 16,000 THB

Around the city, not through it

From Suvarnabhumi, your chauffeur typically follows the southern section of the outer ring road, Motorway 9, crossing the Chao Phraya and joining Rama II Road heading south-west. From there the route continues onto Phetkasem Road, Highway 4, through Phetchaburi to Hua Hin.

The distance is approximately 230 km and the journey usually takes around three to three and a half hours. Rama II has seen long-running roadworks, so your chauffeur will choose the best approach on the day, using the ring road to avoid central Bangkok entirely.

Meet and greet

We monitor your flight from the moment you share your flight number. Your chauffeur waits in the Level 2 arrivals hall with your name on a sign, takes care of your luggage and leads you to the car. We confirm the meeting point on WhatsApp before you fly.

Fast-track immigration and the VIP buggy are available as add-ons at Suvarnabhumi, which can save valuable time at the start of a long transfer. On a three-hour drive, every minute saved in the terminal counts.

Comfort for a long transfer

After a long-haul flight, three hours is a considerable distance. An Alphard or Vellfire, with reclining captain's chairs and space to stretch out, makes the difference between arriving exhausted and arriving ready for dinner by the sea. Your chauffeur is happy to stop at a service station or a café in Phetchaburi whenever you need a break.

Golfers will appreciate that the MPVs take clubs and suitcases together, and families find the sliding doors and generous cabin far easier than a saloon.

If you land late at night, the roads are clear and the journey can be quicker, but some guests prefer to break it with a night near the airport and continue in daylight. Either way, tell us your preference on WhatsApp and we will plan the timing.

Price and booking

This transfer is priced from our Hua Hin rate, with fuel, expressway tolls and the chauffeur included in one fixed price shown in the rate table below. Book on WhatsApp with your flight details and hotel address.

For your departure, allow around three and a half hours from Hua Hin to Suvarnabhumi, plus your airline's check-in time, and a little more on Sunday afternoons, when weekend traffic returns to Bangkok. Your chauffeur will suggest a pick-up time when you confirm.

If you are staying in Cha-am or Pranburi instead, the same airport transfer applies with a short adjustment to the timing.

Highlights

  • Bypasses central Bangkok via the ring road
  • Rama II and Phetkasem Highway south
  • Name-sign meet and greet at arrivals
  • Reclining MPV seats for a long transfer
  • Fixed price, fuel and tolls included

Tips

  • Book fast-track for a quicker start to a long onward journey.
  • Pack a small bag of essentials in the cabin for the three-hour drive.
  • For departures, allow extra time on Sunday afternoons when traffic returns to Bangkok.
  • Consider a night in Bangkok if you land very late and prefer not to drive on.

How long does it take from Suvarnabhumi to Hua Hin?

Usually around three to three and a half hours for approximately 230 km, depending on traffic on Rama II.

Do we have to drive through central Bangkok?

No. Your chauffeur uses the outer ring road to skirt the city and join Rama II Road heading south.

Can we stop on the way?

Of course. Your chauffeur can stop for refreshments or a short break whenever you wish.

Is Don Mueang a better airport for Hua Hin?

The drive times are similar. Choose whichever suits your flights; we cover both.
