Airport Transfer · 190 km · 2.5–3 hrs

From the runway to the rainforest

Khao Yai is one of the most rewarding first stops in Thailand: cool air, forest views and resorts designed for slowing down. From Suvarnabhumi, your chauffeur heads north-east around the city, so you leave the airport behind and arrive in the hills in under three hours.

BKK arrivals≈ 190 km2.5–3 hrsFrom 16,000 THB

Bypassing Bangkok

From the airport, your chauffeur takes the eastern section of the outer ring road, Motorway 9, north to join the main highways towards Saraburi, then follows Mittraphap Road, Highway 2, to Pak Chong at the foot of Khao Yai. The distance is approximately 190 km and the journey usually takes around two and a half to three hours.

The ring road keeps you clear of central Bangkok, which is especially valuable at rush hour. The main pinch points are Friday evenings and long weekends, when city residents head for their country houses.

Arrival and meet and greet

We monitor your flight once you share your flight number, and your chauffeur waits in the Level 2 arrivals hall with a name sign. Fast-track immigration and the VIP buggy are available at Suvarnabhumi as add-ons to your car booking, a sensible addition before a longer onward drive.

Your luggage is loaded for you, and an Alphard or Vellfire takes four large suitcases with room to spare. If you are bringing hiking gear or a pram, mention it on WhatsApp and we will match the vehicle.

Arriving in Khao Yai

Khao Yai's resorts are spread over a wide area, from Thanarat Road leading to the national park to the vineyards and valleys around Mu Si and Phaya Yen. Your chauffeur delivers you to the door, which matters here because taxis are few and many resorts sit on quiet country lanes.

Plan to enter the national park on your first morning, when wildlife is most active. If you would like your chauffeur to stay for a day of exploring, ask about the day-trip rate on our Khao Yai page.

Many resorts can arrange a private safari jeep or guide for the park, and your chauffeur is happy to share local recommendations for restaurants, vineyards and farm cafés on the way in.

Price and booking

This transfer is priced from our Khao Yai rate, with fuel, tolls and the chauffeur included in one fixed price shown in the rate table below. Book on WhatsApp with your flight number and resort address.

For your departure, allow around three hours from Khao Yai to Suvarnabhumi, plus your airline's check-in time, and leave extra margin on Sunday afternoons, when traffic back towards Bangkok is heaviest.

Travellers arriving late in the evening sometimes prefer a night near the airport and a relaxed morning drive, which also brings you into the hills in daylight. We can arrange either.

Highlights

  • Outer ring road avoids central Bangkok
  • Mittraphap Highway to Pak Chong
  • Name-sign meet and greet at arrivals
  • Door-to-door to rural resorts
  • Fixed price with tolls included

Tips

  • Arrive in daylight if you can; rural roads near resorts are dark at night.
  • Pack a light jumper for cool evenings, especially from November to February.
  • Avoid Friday evening arrivals if possible; weekend traffic is heaviest then.
  • Visit the national park on your first morning for the best wildlife sightings.

How long is the drive from Suvarnabhumi to Khao Yai?

Usually around two and a half to three hours for approximately 190 km, avoiding central Bangkok.

Is it better to fly into Don Mueang for Khao Yai?

Both airports work well. Don Mueang is on the north side of the city, while Suvarnabhumi connects via the outer ring road.

Can the chauffeur stay with us in Khao Yai?

Yes. Ask about the day-trip rate or a multi-day arrangement on WhatsApp.

Can I add fast-track?

Yes. Fast-track and VIP buggy add-ons are available at Suvarnabhumi with your car booking.
