Airport Transfer · 160 km · 2–2.5 hrs

Suvarnabhumi to Rayong, without delay

Rayong is a short, direct run from Suvarnabhumi, which sits on the eastern side of Bangkok. Your chauffeur meets you at arrivals and heads straight down Motorway 7 to the beaches, industrial estates or the pier for Koh Samet.

BKK arrivals≈ 160 km2–2.5 hrsFrom 16,000 THB

The route

From the airport, your chauffeur joins Motorway 7 east towards Chonburi and Laem Chabang, then continues along the motorway or Highway 36 to bypass Pattaya and reach Rayong. The distance is approximately 160 km, and the journey usually takes around two to two and a half hours.

Because the route never enters central Bangkok, it is mostly unaffected by city rush hours. Allow a little extra time on Friday evenings and holiday weekends, when the whole Eastern Seaboard is busier.

For business arrivals

Many travellers on this route are heading to Map Ta Phut and the industrial estates of the Eastern Economic Corridor. If your flight lands in the morning, your chauffeur can take you straight to a site meeting, wait during it and continue to your hotel afterwards.

Choose a Camry for a discreet solo arrival or an Alphard Executive if you would like to review documents or take calls from a reclining seat. For visiting teams, several vehicles can travel together and arrive at the same time.

Your chauffeur is familiar with estate gate procedures and visitor car parks, so share the estate name and building when you book. That small detail means you arrive at the right entrance, not the perimeter road, and walk into your meeting composed and on time.

For the beach and Koh Samet

Leisure travellers often head for the beaches at Mae Phim and Mae Ramphueng, or to Ban Phe Pier for boats to Koh Samet. If you are catching a boat, share your boat time and resort details, and your chauffeur will drop you at the right pier with a comfortable margin.

Regular ferries become less frequent later in the afternoon, so an early or midday arrival at Suvarnabhumi suits the island best. If you land late, a night in Rayong and a morning boat is usually the more relaxed choice.

Meet and greet, price and booking

We monitor your flight, and your chauffeur waits in the arrivals hall on Level 2 with your name on a sign. Fast-track and the VIP buggy are available at Suvarnabhumi as add-ons to your car booking.

The transfer is priced from our Rayong rate, with fuel, tolls and the chauffeur included in one fixed price; see the rate table below. Book on WhatsApp with your flight number and destination address.

For departures, allow around two and a half hours from Rayong to the airport, plus check-in time.

Tips

  • Heading to Koh Samet? Aim to reach Ban Phe by mid-afternoon for ferries.
  • Keep visitor passes or host contacts ready for industrial estate gates.
  • Add fast-track if you have a meeting soon after landing.
  • For departures, leave Rayong about four and a half hours before an international flight.
