Suvarnabhumi arrivals & departures

From the Gate to Your Car

Suvarnabhumi is one of Asia's largest terminals, and after a long flight the walk to immigration can feel endless. Our fast-track and VIP buggy service brings you through the airport with a dedicated escort and hands you straight to your waiting SiamDrive chauffeur.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Arrival fast-track

On arrival, a member of the fast-track team meets you after you leave the aircraft and accompanies you through the terminal. You are guided to the priority immigration lane, assisted through baggage reclaim and customs, and walked out to the arrivals hall, where your chauffeur is waiting.

The value is not only time saved. It is having someone beside you who knows the building, answers your questions and makes sure the handover to your car happens without you having to look for anyone.

Queues at Suvarnabhumi immigration vary enormously with the time of day and the number of flights landing together. Fast-track takes that uncertainty out of your arrival, which matters most when you have a meeting soon after landing, tired children in tow or simply no wish to stand in line after a night flight.

The VIP electric buggy

Suvarnabhumi's piers are long. The VIP electric buggy collects you near the gate and drives you through the terminal towards immigration, which is especially welcome for older travellers, families with young children and anyone who would rather not walk the distance after a long-haul flight.

Each buggy seats a maximum of two guests. Larger parties simply book one buggy for every two travellers, and children under three ride free. Buggy service can be combined with arrival fast-track for the smoothest possible start to your stay.

Departure fast-track

Leaving Bangkok, your chauffeur drops you at the terminal where the fast-track team is ready to meet you. They help you through check-in and accompany you through the priority lanes for security and immigration, so you reach the departures area with time to relax rather than queue.

It is a calm, orderly way to end a trip — and a particularly good choice if you are flying out at a busy time of day. Your chauffeur times the drop-off so you are neither rushing nor waiting around, and the fast-track team is told exactly when to expect you.

Available with your SiamDrive car

Fast-track and the VIP buggy are only bookable together with a SiamDrive car booking. Because the same team arranges both, your chauffeur knows exactly when you will emerge and the fast-track team knows exactly who is collecting you. That single plan is what keeps the handover seamless.

Our fast-track service operates at Suvarnabhumi. Prices for arrival, departure and buggy service are listed in our rate table and confirmed with your car booking. Arriving at Don Mueang or U-Tapao instead? Your chauffeur will still meet you in the arrivals hall; fast-track is simply not part of the arrangement there.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Can I book fast-track without a car?

No. Fast-track and the VIP buggy are only available together with a SiamDrive car booking, which allows us to manage your arrival or departure from start to finish.

How many people fit in one buggy?

Each buggy seats a maximum of two guests. For larger parties, book one buggy for every two travellers. Children under three travel free.

Which airports offer your fast-track service?

Our fast-track and VIP buggy service operates at Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK). Transfers from Don Mueang and U-Tapao are available without fast-track.

Does fast-track include help with luggage?

On arrival, the team assists you through baggage reclaim and out to the arrivals hall, where your chauffeur takes over the luggage and loads it into the car.

Is fast-track worth it on departure?

If you are flying at a busy time, travelling with family or simply prefer not to queue, departure fast-track makes the last hour of your trip noticeably calmer.
