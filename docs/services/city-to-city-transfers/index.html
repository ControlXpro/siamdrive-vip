Fixed fares across Thailand

Door to Door, City to City

Leave the bus station and the airport shuttle to someone else. A SiamDrive chauffeur collects you from your door in Bangkok and delivers you to your hotel, villa or pier — at a fixed fare that already includes fuel and tolls.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Where we drive

Our most travelled routes run east to Pattaya, Sriracha, Rayong and Ban Phe for Koh Samet; south-west to Hua Hin, Cha-am and Pranburi; north-east to Khao Yai and Nakhon Nayok; and west to Kanchanaburi and the floating markets. Every route has its own fixed fare per vehicle in our rate table.

For further destinations, such as Chanthaburi or the Laem Ngop pier for Koh Chang, we quote on request so the price reflects the actual distance and timing.

Transfers also run in the other direction and between resorts. Collection from a Pattaya hotel for a flight at Suvarnabhumi, or a move from Hua Hin to a Bangkok riverside hotel, is priced on exactly the same fixed basis.

Comfort over a long distance

A three-hour journey feels very different in a quiet, air-conditioned car with room to stretch. Ask for a coffee stop, a break for the children or a detour to pick up a friend, and your chauffeur will build it into the drive.

Your luggage travels with you, your route is chosen by someone who drives it regularly, and you arrive at the door rather than at a terminal.

Choose the vehicle to match the party. A Toyota Camry is a calm, efficient choice for one or two travellers; an Alphard or Vellfire gives up to five guests their own space and takes four large suitcases. For a group with more luggage than seats, a second car is often the most comfortable answer.

Pattaya and the Eastern Seaboard

Fares to Pattaya, Sriracha, Bang Saen and other Eastern Seaboard stops include up to ten hours with the car. That makes a same-day visit — a lunch meeting, a site inspection, an afternoon at the beach — straightforward, with the same chauffeur bringing you back to Bangkok. If the day runs beyond the included hours, additional time is charged at the overtime rate in our rate table.

Return journeys and overnight stays

If you are staying a few days, book your return transfer at the same time and we will collect you from your hotel on the agreed date. If you would like the same driver and car to stay with you overnight, an overnight supplement covers the chauffeur's accommodation; we confirm it with your quote.

One-way, return or multi-stop — tell us how you are travelling and we will price it clearly before you commit. If you are combining a transfer with a sightseeing stop along the way, such as Ayutthaya en route north or the floating markets en route to Hua Hin, we can quote that as a single journey.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Is the fare per person or per car?

Per car. The fare covers the vehicle, chauffeur, fuel and tolls, whether you travel alone or with a full party.

Can you take us to the pier for Koh Samet or Koh Chang?

Yes. We drive to Ban Phe pier for Koh Samet at a fixed fare, and to Laem Ngop for Koh Chang on a quote-on-request basis. The ferry itself is booked separately.

Can we stop on the way?

Of course. Short comfort stops are part of the journey. If you want a longer visit along the route, mention it when booking so we can price the time correctly.

Can the driver stay with us overnight?

Yes. An overnight supplement covers the chauffeur's accommodation, and we include it in your quote so there are no surprises.

Do you collect from outside Bangkok?

Yes. We regularly collect in Pattaya, Hua Hin and other resort towns for transfers back to Bangkok or to the airports.
