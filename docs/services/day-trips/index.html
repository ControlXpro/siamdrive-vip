Ten-hour private day trips

Leave the City, Keep the Comfort

Thailand's ancient capitals, national parks and floating markets are all within a morning's drive of Bangkok. A private day trip gives you a chauffeur and car for ten hours, so you can explore at your own pace and be home for dinner.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Ten hours, entirely your own

There is no coach to catch and no group to wait for. Your chauffeur collects you from your hotel, drives you to your first stop and waits while you explore, then moves on whenever you are ready.

Each destination carries a fixed day-trip fare per vehicle in our rate table, with driver, fuel and tolls included. If you choose to linger, extra time is charged at the overtime rate.

The car becomes your base for the day. Leave a change of clothes, a bag of shopping or a sleeping child's blanket on the seat; the air-conditioning will be running when you return from a hot temple courtyard, and cold water is never far away at the next stop.

Where our guests like to go

Ayutthaya, the former royal capital, pairs well with Bang Pa-In for a day of temples and history. Damnoen Saduak and Amphawa make an early-morning floating-market outing, while Kanchanaburi offers the Bridge over the River Kwai, the war cemeteries and waterfalls further out.

For greenery and fresh air, Khao Yai combines national-park scenery with vineyards and farm cafés. Pattaya and the Eastern Seaboard are ideal for a day by the sea.

Closer to the city, Nakhon Pathom's great chedi and the riverside town of Chachoengsao make relaxed half-day or full-day outings, and Lopburi offers Khmer ruins alongside its famous monkeys. Each destination has its own fare tier, so the price is clear before you choose.

Planning the day well

Leaving Bangkok early avoids the worst of the outbound traffic and gets you to popular sites before the heat and the crowds. Tell us what matters most — photographs at sunrise, a long lunch, time for the children to rest — and we will suggest a realistic order of stops. A well-paced day usually means two or three main stops rather than five rushed ones, with lunch somewhere worth sitting down for and enough time on the way home to rest before the evening.

Our chauffeurs are drivers rather than licensed tour guides. They know the roads, the car parks and the practicalities; for in-depth commentary at a historic site, we recommend booking a local guide there.

Families travelling with small children or older relatives often prefer an Alphard or Vellfire, where the sliding doors and captain's chairs make getting in and out easy and a nap between stops genuinely restful. Couples and solo travellers are perfectly comfortable in the Camry, and the Cayenne S turns the drive itself into part of the occasion.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

What time should we leave Bangkok?

For most destinations, an early start is best. Floating markets are liveliest in the morning, and leaving early avoids outbound traffic. We will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Are entrance fees included?

No. The fare covers the vehicle, chauffeur, fuel and tolls. Entrance tickets, meals and any local guides are paid separately.

Can we combine two destinations in one day?

Often, yes — Ayutthaya with Bang Pa-In, or Damnoen Saduak with Amphawa, work well together. Ask us and we will tell you honestly what fits into ten hours.

Will the driver guide us around the sites?

Your chauffeur will drop you at the right entrance and advise on practicalities, but they are not licensed guides. Local guides can be hired at most major sites.

What if we want to stay longer?

Let your chauffeur know. Extra time is charged at the overtime rate in our rate table, subject to the driver's availability.
