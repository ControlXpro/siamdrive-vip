Sports teams · Federations · Corporate delegations · Conferences

Every Arrival, Every Delegate, Coordinated

Moving a delegation is not one journey but dozens: arrivals on different flights, daily movements between hotel and venue, official dinners and departures spread over several days. SiamDrive plans and runs the whole programme, with one point of contact for your organisation.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Built for groups that arrive in waves

Sports teams, federations, corporate delegations and conference speakers rarely land together. An athlete arrives from Europe at dawn, the coaching staff connect through Doha in the afternoon, officials fly in the next day. We track each arrival separately, assign the right vehicle to each flight and confirm every pickup back to your coordinator.

Your organisation sends one arrivals list; we turn it into a pickup schedule, update it as flights change and report back as each group reaches the hotel. Vehicles are matched to each group: a Camry for a single official, an Alphard or Vellfire for small groups with luggage, and several vehicles moving together for a full squad. Where a team travels with more bags than seats, we plan an additional vehicle for luggage so that nobody arrives cramped.

Fast-track and buggies for every arrival

At Suvarnabhumi, each arrival can be met with fast-track through the terminal and, where useful, the VIP electric buggy. Buggies seat a maximum of two guests each, so we plan the number of buggies per flight around the size of each arriving group; children under three ride free.

Fast-track is only bookable together with a SiamDrive car, which is precisely what makes it work for delegations: the fast-track team knows which vehicle is waiting, and the chauffeur knows exactly who is coming through.

Through the programme

Once everyone has arrived, we cover the daily rhythm of the visit: hotel to training ground or conference venue, stadium and match-day movements, site visits, official dinners and sightseeing on rest days. Vehicles can be booked by the hour, by the day or as fixed transfers, depending on how structured your programme is.

For high-profile movements, motorcycle escort riders keep multi-vehicle convoys together through Bangkok traffic, and suited, English-speaking bodyguards can accompany principals and key officials. Departures are handled with the same care as arrivals, staggered to match each flight.

Invoicing and coordination for organisations

Federations, clubs, companies and event organisers need clear paperwork, and finance teams need it early. We confirm the full transport plan in writing, issue invoices to your organisation and accept payment by bank transfer, with payment details confirmed on WhatsApp.

Throughout the visit, your coordinator has a single contact for every change — a new arrival, an extra vehicle, an early departure — and every adjustment is confirmed in the same thread. At the end of the visit, the final invoice reflects what was actually used, so your finance team can reconcile it against the approved plan without chasing details.

We are equally comfortable working alongside your own event agency or ground handler, taking on the transport element while they manage the wider programme.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Can you manage arrivals on many different flights?

Yes. That is the core of the service. We track each flight separately, assign vehicles per arrival and confirm each pickup to your coordinator.

How many buggies do we need for a team?

Each buggy carries a maximum of two guests, and children under three ride free. We calculate the number of buggies for each arriving group when we build the plan.

Can you invoice our federation or company?

Yes. We issue invoices to organisations and accept payment by bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can you transport equipment as well as people?

Tell us what equipment is travelling. Personal kit and standard luggage fit in our vehicles; for bulky team equipment, we will discuss the most practical arrangement.

Do you provide security for officials?

Yes. Suited, English-speaking bodyguards and motorcycle escort riders can be added, booked together with SiamDrive vehicles.
