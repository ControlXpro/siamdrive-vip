Bangkok · Pattaya · Golf days out

Tee Time, Handled

Thailand has some of the finest golf in Asia, and many of its best courses lie just beyond Bangkok's traffic. A SiamDrive chauffeur collects you and your clubs, drives you to the first tee and is waiting when you come off the eighteenth.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

From your hotel to the first tee

Golf days start early, and the last thing you want before a morning round is a taxi that cannot fit your bag. Your chauffeur arrives at the agreed time, loads the clubs and takes the quickest route to the course, timed so you have a relaxed few minutes at the range before you tee off.

Courses within and around Bangkok are reached comfortably with hourly hire, which keeps the car with you while you play. Outside the city, we quote a fixed outskirt or day-trip fare, so you know the full cost before you book the round.

Clubs, shoes and luggage

Golf bags take up luggage space, so the number of players and bags decides the vehicle. A Toyota Camry suits one or two golfers with their clubs. The Alphard and Vellfire offer the most flexible cabin for small groups, but four full-size tour bags plus four players is a tight fit in any single car.

For a four-ball with full bags, two vehicles are often the more comfortable answer — or one MPV for the players and a second car for the equipment. Tell us exactly what you are bringing and we will recommend honestly rather than squeeze everything in.

Golf in Pattaya and the Eastern Seaboard

The Eastern Seaboard is home to a dense cluster of well-regarded courses around Pattaya, Sriracha and Bang Saen. Our Eastern Seaboard fares include up to ten hours with the car, which is enough for the drive, a full round, lunch at the clubhouse and the journey home without watching the clock.

If you are staying in Pattaya for a golf break, your chauffeur can collect you from your hotel each morning, or we can arrange a single transfer down from Bangkok and another back at the end of the trip. Flying into U-Tapao instead of Bangkok brings you even closer to the Eastern Seaboard courses, and we collect from there too.

The nineteenth hole

With a chauffeur waiting, there is no need to keep an eye on what you drink at the clubhouse or argue over who drives home. Settle the day's bets over lunch, then sink into the back seat and let someone else deal with the expressway.

For corporate golf days, we can coordinate several vehicles for a group of players arriving from different hotels, all arriving in good time for a shotgun start. Add arrival fast-track at Suvarnabhumi for players flying in, and your golf break can begin the moment you step off the aircraft rather than at the back of the immigration queue.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

How many golf bags fit in each car?

A Camry suits one or two golfers with clubs. The Alphard and Vellfire are more flexible, but a full four-ball with tour bags usually travels more comfortably in two vehicles. Tell us what you are bringing and we will advise.

Will the driver wait while we play?

Yes. With hourly hire or a day fare, your chauffeur stays at the course and drives you back after your round.

Do you arrange tee times or green fees?

No. We handle the transport; tee times, green fees, caddies and buggies are booked with the course directly.

Can you collect us from Pattaya each morning?

Yes. If you are based in Pattaya, we can arrange daily collections from your hotel to the courses nearby.

What time should we leave Bangkok for an early tee time?

It depends on the course and the day of the week. Share your tee time and we will suggest a pickup that leaves comfortable margin for traffic.
