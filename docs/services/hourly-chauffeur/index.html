As-directed hire · 5 or 10 hours

Your Driver, Your Day, Your Pace

Some days do not fit a single journey. Book a chauffeur and vehicle for five or ten hours and go wherever the day takes you, with your car waiting outside every stop.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Book a block of time, not a route

As-directed hire means the car is yours for the hours you book. Tell your chauffeur where to go next and they take you there — a meeting in Sathorn, lunch in Thonglor, a fitting in Siam and dinner by the river, all without hailing a single taxi.

Choose a five-hour block for a busy afternoon or a ten-hour block for a full day. If the day runs longer, additional hours are charged at the overtime rate shown in our rate table, so there is never any guesswork.

Because you are booking time rather than distance, the number of stops makes no difference to the price. Ten short hops across Sukhumvit cost the same as one long drive to the riverside, which makes hourly hire the natural choice whenever your plans are not yet fixed.

Made for a city with many stops

Bangkok rewards local knowledge. Your chauffeur knows where to set down at busy hotel forecourts and shopping centres, and how to work around the heaviest traffic. While you are inside, the car stays close, air-conditioned and ready.

That means no waiting on a hot pavement, no re-explaining addresses and no carrying bags from one appointment to the next. Anything you leave in the car stays with your driver.

When a meeting overruns or a restaurant reservation moves, a quick WhatsApp message to your chauffeur is all it takes. They reposition the car, adjust the route and are at the door when you step out, so a change of plan never becomes a problem.

Inside Bangkok and just beyond

Five- and ten-hour hire covers journeys within Bangkok. If your day reaches further — Samut Prakan, Nakhon Pathom or other points within roughly seventy kilometres — we quote it as an outskirt day, and longer excursions are covered by our day-trip fares.

Driver, fuel and tolls are included in every hourly booking, so the only figure to keep in mind is the time. Your driver finds a place to wait near each stop and returns to the entrance when you call, leaving you free to step in and out of the car without a second thought.

Add security or an assistant

Hourly hire combines naturally with our other services. A suited bodyguard can join you for the same hours, and a personal assistant can help with translation, reservations and errands while your chauffeur handles the driving.

Both are arranged in the same WhatsApp conversation as your car, priced clearly from our rate table and briefed on your schedule in advance. Bodyguards are only bookable together with a SiamDrive car, which is exactly what hourly hire provides.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Do I need to give you a full itinerary in advance?

No. A rough outline helps your chauffeur plan, but as-directed hire is designed for changes. You can add or drop stops as the day goes on.

What happens if I need the car for longer?

Simply tell your chauffeur or message us. Extra time is charged per hour at the overtime rate in our rate table, subject to the driver's availability.

Can I start in Bangkok and finish somewhere else?

Hourly hire is designed for Bangkok. If your day ends outside the city, ask us for an outskirt, day-trip or one-way transfer fare instead, which is usually better value.

When does the time start?

Your booked hours begin at the agreed pickup time at your address, so you are not paying for the driver's journey to reach you.

Can the driver run errands while I am in a meeting?

Your chauffeur stays with the car and can collect a parcel or a guest at another address if you ask. For errands that need someone on foot, add a personal assistant to your booking.
