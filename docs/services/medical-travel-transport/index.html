For medical travellers & their companions

Calm, Comfortable Rides to Appointments

Bangkok's private hospitals attract patients from around the world. If you are travelling for treatment, the journeys in between — airport, hotel, consultations, follow-ups — should feel calm and easy. SiamDrive provides gentle, punctual private transport for patients and the people who accompany them.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Getting to every appointment on time

Medical schedules are precise. A scan at nine, a consultation at eleven, a follow-up the next morning. Your chauffeur plans around Bangkok's traffic, arrives early and drops you at the correct hospital entrance, so you never have to rush or worry about finding your way.

Whether you are visiting one of the large private hospitals along Sukhumvit, a dental clinic in Silom or a specialist practice elsewhere in the city, the car waits nearby and is ready the moment you are discharged.

Your treatment journey usually begins at the airport. We collect you at Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang and take you directly to your hotel or hospital, and at Suvarnabhumi you can add fast-track and the VIP electric buggy so that the long walk through the terminal is not the first challenge of your trip. Departures after treatment are arranged with the same care.

Comfort during recovery

After a procedure, comfort matters. The Toyota Alphard and Vellfire have wide power sliding doors, a low step and spacious captain's chairs, which make getting in and out easier for patients who are tired, stiff or recovering. The Alphard 40 Executive goes further, with reclining lounge seats and leg rests for a more restful ride.

Your chauffeur drives smoothly, helps with bags and doors and adjusts the cabin temperature to suit you. If you need to stop on the way, just say. Discretion matters here too: your chauffeur will not ask about your treatment, and your appointments remain your own business.

For patients and companions

Many medical travellers come with a partner or family member. Hourly hire gives the whole party flexibility: the patient attends their appointment while a companion runs errands, collects prescriptions or takes a break, all with the same car on call.

For longer treatment programmes, a monthly private driver package provides a dedicated driver and Alphard seven days a week. A personal assistant can also help with translation, pharmacy visits and practical tasks in Bangkok.

What we are, and what we are not

SiamDrive is a private chauffeur service, not a medical provider. Our vehicles are not ambulances, and our chauffeurs are not medical staff. We do not provide medical equipment, monitoring or care during the journey.

If you need a stretcher, oxygen or medical supervision while travelling, please arrange transport through your hospital. In an emergency in Thailand, call 1669 for emergency medical services. Being clear about this means that when you travel with us, you know exactly what to expect: a comfortable car, a careful driver and nothing more complicated than that.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Is this an ambulance service?

No. SiamDrive provides private chauffeured transport only. We do not provide medical staff, equipment or care. For medical transport, please contact your hospital; in an emergency, call 1669.

Which vehicle is easiest to get in and out of?

The Alphard and Vellfire, with their wide sliding doors and spacious seats, are the easiest for most patients. The Alphard 40 Executive adds reclining seats with leg rests.

Can you take a folding wheelchair?

A folding wheelchair can usually be carried in the luggage space of our MPVs. Please mention it when booking so we can plan the space alongside your other luggage.

Can the driver wait during a long appointment?

Yes. With hourly hire, your chauffeur stays nearby and is ready as soon as you are discharged.

Do you offer longer-term arrangements?

Yes. Monthly private driver packages provide a dedicated driver and Alphard seven days a week, ten hours a day, which suits extended treatment and recovery stays.
