Rooftops · Restaurants · Late nights

Your Night Out, Driven Home Safely

Bangkok after dark is one of the world's great nights out — rooftop bars above the skyline, celebrated restaurants, speakeasies in Thonglor and clubs that keep going long after midnight. A private chauffeur lets you enjoy every part of it and get home without a second thought.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

A car that is always waiting

Book a chauffeur for five or ten hours and your car follows you through the evening: dinner in Sathorn, cocktails on a rooftop in Silom, a late bar in Thonglor. When you are ready to move on, a message brings the car to the door.

No searching for a taxi at closing time, no ride-hailing surge, no struggling to explain an address after a long dinner. The car is yours, the driver knows the route home, and you step straight from the pavement into a cool, quiet cabin.

Where the evening goes

Our chauffeurs know the city at night — the hotel rooftops along Sukhumvit and the riverside, the restaurant rows of Thonglor and Ekkamai, the bar streets of Silom and the late venues that only open their doors after eleven. They know where cars can wait and which entrances are easiest to reach.

If you are planning a celebration — a birthday, a bachelor or bachelorette night, a reunion with friends — tell us the plan in advance and we will suggest how to sequence the evening so the travel between venues feels like part of the fun rather than an interruption.

Dress codes, guest lists and reservations are yours to arrange, but your chauffeur can advise on practical details such as which venues are easier to reach by car.

Safety at the end of the night

The riskiest moment of any night out is often the journey home. A familiar car, a driver you have already met and a confirmed route take away the uncertainty of flagging down a stranger at three in the morning. Anything you leave in the car — a jacket, a shopping bag, a phone — stays safely with your chauffeur.

For guests who want an extra layer of reassurance, a suited, English-speaking bodyguard can join you for the evening. Bodyguards are only bookable together with a SiamDrive car, so your chauffeur and bodyguard work as one team from the first venue to your front door.

Arranging the evening

Choose the vehicle to suit the group: a Camry for two, an Alphard or Vellfire for up to five friends, or the Porsche Cayenne S when the arrival matters. If the night runs beyond your booked hours, additional time is charged at the overtime rate in our rate table — just let your driver know.

A short note on timing: Bangkok's traffic eases considerably later in the evening, so the drive between venues after ten is usually quick. Start dinner a little earlier, and the rest of the night tends to flow on its own. Your chauffeur will happily suggest a sensible order if you share the places on your list.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Can the driver wait outside clubs until late?

Yes. Your chauffeur stays with you for the booked hours. If the night runs longer, extra hours are charged at the overtime rate.

Can you drop different guests at different hotels?

Of course. Give your chauffeur the addresses and they will plan the most sensible route home for everyone in the car.

Is it safe to leave bags in the car?

Your chauffeur stays with the vehicle while you are inside, so shopping and personal items remain with the car. We still recommend keeping passports and valuables with you.

Can I book a bodyguard for a night out?

Yes. A suited, English-speaking bodyguard can accompany you, booked together with your SiamDrive car.

Which car suits a group of friends?

The Alphard and Vellfire seat up to five guests in comfort. For larger groups, we can arrange a second vehicle.
