An Extra Pair of Hands in Bangkok

Sometimes what makes a trip easier is not another car but another person. A SiamDrive personal assistant spends ten hours with you in Bangkok — translating, organising, helping with the children and taking care of the details so you do not have to.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

A capable companion for your day

Your assistant is part travel buddy, part fixer. They can help you order at a local restaurant, bargain politely at a market, find the right pharmacy, call ahead to confirm a reservation or explain to a tailor exactly what you have in mind.

For first-time visitors, that local fluency turns a city that can feel overwhelming into one that is easy and enjoyable. For returning guests, it means getting more done with less effort.

Assistants are equally useful on working trips. They can collect documents, wait in for a delivery at your hotel, accompany you to a government office or bank appointment to help with Thai paperwork, or simply keep track of a busy schedule so nothing slips.

Family support and nanny care

Travelling with children is a joy, and also hard work. Your assistant can act as a nanny for the day: keeping the children entertained, accompanying them to a playground or aquarium, and giving parents a few hours for a meeting, a spa appointment or a quiet lunch.

Let us know the children's ages and any routines or needs when you book, and we will brief your assistant accordingly. If you are booking several days, ask for the same assistant throughout where possible, so the children come to know a familiar, friendly face during their time in Bangkok.

Ten hours in Bangkok

The service is booked as a ten-hour day within Bangkok. If your plans run later, additional hours are charged at the overtime rate in our rate table. Your assistant can travel with you in your SiamDrive car, keeping the whole day coordinated: your chauffeur drives, your assistant handles everything on foot.

Typical days might include a morning of errands and appointments, an afternoon exploring markets and temples, and help preparing for an evening event — or a combination entirely of your own design. Tell us roughly what you have in mind when booking, and we will match you with an assistant suited to the day.

Strictly professional

Our personal assistant service is strictly professional companionship and assistance. Assistants are there to help you travel, work and look after your family with ease; the boundaries of the role are clear, and we ask every client to respect them.

If you are unsure whether something falls within the service, simply ask us on WhatsApp before you book. We are happy to explain what your assistant can help with and suggest alternatives for anything outside the role. Clear expectations on both sides are what make the service genuinely relaxing for you and comfortable for your assistant.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

What can a personal assistant help with?

Translation, reservations, shopping, errands, sightseeing, accompanying you to appointments and looking after children. It is strictly professional companionship and assistance.

Can the assistant look after my children alone?

Yes, nanny care is part of the service. Tell us the children's ages, routines and any allergies or needs when booking so your assistant is properly prepared.

Does the service include a car?

The assistant is booked separately from transport. Most clients pair it with a SiamDrive chauffeur so the whole day is coordinated, but it is not required.

Can my assistant travel outside Bangkok?

The service is designed as a ten-hour day in Bangkok. For plans outside the city, ask us on WhatsApp and we will advise what is possible.

What languages do assistants speak?

Assistants speak Thai and English. If you need another language, mention it when booking and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
