For the couple, the family and the guests

Arrive Beautifully on Your Day

On your wedding day, the car carries more than passengers. It carries the moment you arrive, the photographs on the steps and the quiet minutes between ceremony and celebration. SiamDrive provides elegant vehicles and composed chauffeurs so every journey feels part of the occasion.

Fixed price24/7 conciergeEnglish-speaking

Choosing the wedding car

The Porsche Cayenne S, with its red-leather cabin and unmistakable presence, makes a striking arrival for the couple. The Rowen Vellfire Z offers statement styling with space for a flowing dress and a bridesmaid or two. For a calm, spacious ride where comfort matters most, the Alphard 40 Executive is our flagship.

Many couples choose one car for themselves and a second for parents or the bridal party, so everyone arrives together and in style.

Think about the dress as well as the photographs. A full gown and a long train travel far more comfortably in a spacious MPV with wide sliding doors than in a low saloon, while a sleek SUV suits a more tailored look. If you are unsure, describe your plans and we will suggest the car that will feel best on the day.

Transport for family and guests

Weddings in Bangkok often bring relatives and friends from overseas, which means airport collections, hotel transfers and guests who do not know the city. We can arrange all of it: arrivals at Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang, shuttles between hotel and venue, and late-night returns after the reception.

For destination weddings in Hua Hin, Pattaya or Khao Yai, we offer fixed fares from Bangkok and the airports, so guests can travel straight to the celebration. Older relatives who would rather not walk the length of Suvarnabhumi can be met with fast-track and the VIP buggy, arranged alongside their transfer.

Planning the timings

Thai and international weddings alike run on careful schedules — a morning ceremony, photographs at a second location, an evening reception. Share your timeline and we will plan each movement with enough margin for traffic, so nobody is rushing.

Hourly hire keeps the couple's car on standby throughout the day, ready for the next location or a quiet moment away from the crowd. Extra hours are charged at the overtime rate if celebrations run late. For a convoy of family cars moving between ceremony and reception, motorcycle escort riders can keep every vehicle together through the city.

Finishing touches

If you would like ribbons, flowers or other decoration on the wedding car, let us know when booking and we will confirm what is possible for your chosen vehicle. Your chauffeur will be smartly dressed, will help with the dress and the doors, and will make sure the car is spotless for your photographs. Photographers are welcome to use the car as a backdrop between locations; simply let your chauffeur know where you would like it positioned.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Our concierge replies on WhatsApp, day or night.

Which car is most popular for weddings?

Couples who want a statement often choose the Porsche Cayenne S or the Rowen Vellfire Z. Those who prioritise space and comfort prefer the Alphard 40 Executive.

Can you decorate the car?

Let us know your ideas when booking. We will confirm what decoration is possible for the vehicle you have chosen.

Can you transport our guests as well?

Yes. We arrange airport collections, hotel-to-venue shuttles and late-night returns, coordinated through one WhatsApp conversation.

Do you cover weddings outside Bangkok?

Yes. Destination weddings in Hua Hin, Pattaya, Khao Yai and other locations are covered with fixed transfer fares and day hire.

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as your date is confirmed, especially for the Porsche Cayenne S and Rowen Vellfire Z, which are in demand on popular wedding dates.
